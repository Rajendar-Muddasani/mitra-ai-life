Words and Reading · शब्द और पाठन

Now that you know the letters and the barakhadi, let us learn full words. Each card shows a real picture, the Hindi word, how it sounds in English, and what it means. Say each word aloud with the picture in front of you.

How to use this page

Pick one topic at a time. Look at the picture, say the Hindi word aloud, then say the meaning. Do five words today, the next five tomorrow. Watch the video together at the end of each topic and repeat after Mitra.

Watch and listen — सभी शब्द (all words)

The video shows each card and says the Hindi word twice with its pronunciation and meaning. Pause after each card and say it aloud.
