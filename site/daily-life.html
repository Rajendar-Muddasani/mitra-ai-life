🏪 Ravi · Mobile Shop · Hyderabad

Writing a WhatsApp promotional message

Ravi noticed competitors offering festival discounts and wanted to send a genuine WhatsApp message — not a mass-forward.

PROMPT TO COPY

I run a mobile repair and accessories shop in Hyderabad. Write a short WhatsApp message for Dussehra. Offer 15% off on screen guards and phone covers until Oct 15. Sound genuine and friendly, not like a mass forward.

AI GAVE BACK

🎉 Dussehra Mubarak! This festive season, protect your phone with our premium screen guards and covers — now at 15% off till Oct 15. Visit Ravi Mobile Shop, Hyderabad. Tell your friends! 📱

🍱 Meena · Tiffin Service · Nalgonda

Writing a WhatsApp menu

A housing society asked Meena for her menu. She had never written one and didn't know where to start.

PROMPT TO COPY

I run a home tiffin service in Nalgonda. I make idli (₹40), upma (₹35), pesarattu (₹45), and full meals on request (₹80). I deliver by 8am. Write a simple WhatsApp menu with a catchy tagline. Keep it warm and homely.

AI GAVE BACK

🍽️ Meena's Morning Tiffins — Fresh, Hot, Delivered by 8am

Idli · ₹40 | Upma · ₹35 | Pesarattu · ₹45 | Full Meals · ₹80

📞 Order by 7pm for next-day delivery. Made with love, every morning.

✂️ Suresh · Tailor · Karimnagar

Replying to a customer complaint

A customer complained about wrong measurements. Suresh wanted to apologise professionally without losing them.

PROMPT TO COPY

I am a tailor in Karimnagar. A customer complained that the shirt I stitched has wrong measurements. Write a polite WhatsApp reply that apologises sincerely, offers a free correction, and asks them to come back. Keep it short and warm.

AI GAVE BACK

Dear [Name], I'm truly sorry about the measurement issue. That should never have happened. Please bring the shirt back — I will correct it for free, no questions asked. Thank you for your patience. 🙏

🧣 Divya · Saree Reseller · Warangal

Writing captions for 5 sarees in 3 minutes

Divya sells sarees on WhatsApp and Instagram. Writing one caption per saree was taking 20 minutes. She needed a faster way.

PROMPT TO COPY

I sell sarees on WhatsApp and Instagram. Write 5 short product captions for: 1) Pink Banarasi silk ₹2400, 2) Blue Kanjivaram cotton ₹1800, 3) Yellow pochampally ikat ₹1200, 4) Green chanderi ₹900, 5) Red georgette ₹1500. Each caption: 2 lines, emoji, price, "DM to order".

1. 🌸 Elegant Pink Banarasi Silk — perfect for weddings. ₹2400 | DM to order
2. 💙 Soft Blue Kanjivaram Cotton — lightweight beauty. ₹1800 | DM to order

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI for Daily Life course really free?

Yes. Levels 1 to 10 of AI for Daily Life are free to use. Any future paid support will be shown separately.

Do I need a laptop or computer?

No. Any smartphone with internet works. Lessons are designed mobile-first with simple visuals.

My English is weak. Can I still learn?

Yes. Lessons are written in very simple English, and Telugu versions are rolling out level by level.

Do I need any AI account to start?

You can read the lessons without any account. To try the AI tools shown in lessons, a free ChatGPT or Gemini account is enough.
