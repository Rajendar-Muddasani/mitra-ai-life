Letters and Sounds

Hindi has 12 vowel sounds called swar and 33 consonants called vyanjan. These are the building blocks of every word. Each has a unique shape and a clear sound. Say each one aloud many times before moving on.

Barakhadi

Barakhadi shows what happens when a consonant joins with each vowel sound. Each row is one consonant spoken across all 12 vowel matras — क, का, कि, की, कु, कू, के, कै, को, कौ, कं, कः. Learning one row at a time is the fastest way to read Hindi words.
