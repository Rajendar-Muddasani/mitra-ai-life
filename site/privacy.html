Privacy Policy

Mitra AI Life is built for public learning and practical family use. This page explains what information the public site may collect, why it is collected, and how to ask for changes or removal.

Contact Mitra AI LifeBack to Home

Quick summary

  • Public site first: most visitors can browse without creating an account.
  • Limited collection: we aim to collect only what is needed for analytics, contact replies, or learner access.
  • No guaranteed AI truth: user interactions with AI tools should avoid sensitive personal data.

What information may be collected

Site analytics

We may use tools such as Google Analytics to understand page visits, traffic sources, device types, and broad usage patterns.

Contact details

If you email or contact Mitra AI Life, we may receive your name, email address, role, language preference, and the details you choose to share.

Learning access data

If sign-in, quizzes, or member features are expanded later, we may store learner progress, selected language, and basic usage records needed to run the service.

How information is used

Information is used to improve lessons, respond to enquiries, measure which public pages are useful, and operate future learner access features. Mitra AI Life should stay beginner-first and privacy-aware, especially for students, families, and school use.

What not to share with AI tools

Do not share Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords, medical records, private student records, or any other highly sensitive personal information with public AI tools. AI systems can make mistakes, and prompts may be reviewed by service providers under their own policies.

Third-party services

The public site may rely on third-party services such as GitHub Pages, Amazon S3, Google Analytics, Supabase, and OpenAI-powered features. Those services may process technical data according to their own policies. Mitra AI Life should continue choosing simple, low-risk, low-data approaches wherever possible.

Children and school use

Child-facing and school-facing experiences should avoid unnecessary personal data collection. Parents, schools, and teachers should not submit sensitive student information through public contact or AI prompt fields.

Contact for privacy requests

To ask about data use, correction, or deletion, contact hello@mitraailife.com. As the platform grows, this page should be updated whenever new data flows, payment systems, or learner accounts are introduced.
