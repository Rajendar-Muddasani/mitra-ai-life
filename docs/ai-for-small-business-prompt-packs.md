# AI for Small Business — Reusable Prompt Packs

> Purpose: copy-ready AI prompts for small business owners to use directly in ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or any free AI tool. Keep these short enough to type on a phone.

## How to use these prompts

1. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI chat tool on your phone or laptop.
2. Copy the prompt below.
3. Replace the words in [square brackets] with your actual business details.
4. Read the AI reply and edit it to match your own style before sending.
5. Never paste real customer phone numbers, addresses, or private details.

---

## Pack 1 — Customer reply prompts

### Enquiry reply

```
Write a short WhatsApp reply for a [shop type] in India.
A customer asked: "[customer's question]"
Keep the reply simple, polite, and under 3 lines.
End with how to place an order or contact us.
```

Example:
```
Write a short WhatsApp reply for a home bakery in Hyderabad.
A customer asked: "Do you make custom birthday cakes?"
Keep the reply simple, polite, and under 3 lines.
End with how to place an order or contact us.
```

---

### Follow-up message

```
Write a short follow-up WhatsApp message for a [shop type].
The customer enquired 2 days ago but has not replied.
Keep it warm, not pushy, and under 2 lines.
```

---

### Delay or stock apology

```
Write a polite WhatsApp message for a [shop type].
Tell the customer their order or item is delayed by [number] days.
Keep it under 3 lines. Apologise, explain briefly, and offer a next step.
```

---

### Business-hours reply

```
Write a short WhatsApp auto-reply for a [shop type].
We are closed now. Working hours are [hours].
Keep it friendly and under 2 lines.
```

## Pack 2 — Offers and promotions prompts

### Simple WhatsApp offer

```
Write a short WhatsApp offer message for a [shop type] in India.
Offer: [describe the offer briefly]
Keep it under 4 lines. Use simple language. No exaggerated claims.
End with a clear action: call, reply, or visit.
```

Example:
```
Write a short WhatsApp offer message for a tiffin service in Bengaluru.
Offer: 10% off on monthly subscriptions booked this week.
Keep it under 4 lines. Use simple language. No exaggerated claims.
End with a clear action: call, reply, or visit.
```

---

### Instagram caption

```
Write a short Instagram caption for a [shop type] in India.
Product or service: [describe the product or service]
Tone: warm, local, and simple. Not corporate.
Include one relevant emoji. Keep it under 3 lines.
```

---

### Poster headline

```
Write 3 short poster headline options for a [shop type] in India.
Occasion or offer: [festival or offer name]
Keep each headline under 10 words. Use simple and direct language.
```

---

### Festival promotion message

```
Write a short [festival name] promotion WhatsApp message for a [shop type] in India.
Offer: [describe the offer]
Mention the festival briefly. Keep the message under 4 lines. No fake urgency.
```

## Pack 3 — Review and complaint reply prompts

### Thank a positive review

```
Write a short reply to a positive Google review for a [shop type].
Review says: "[paste review text or describe it]"
Keep the reply under 3 lines. Sound genuine, not corporate.
```

---

### Respond to a negative review

```
Write a calm and professional reply to a negative Google review for a [shop type].
Review says: "[paste review text or describe it]"
Do not be defensive. Acknowledge the issue, apologise briefly, and offer a next step.
Keep it under 4 lines.
```

---

### Handle a WhatsApp complaint

```
Write a calm WhatsApp reply to a complaint for a [shop type].
Customer complaint: "[describe the complaint briefly]"
Acknowledge the issue without argument. Offer a simple resolution.
Keep it under 4 lines.
```

## Pack 4 — Promotion planning prompts

### Weekly message plan

```
Create a simple 5-message WhatsApp plan for a [shop type] in India for one week.
Business goal this week: [describe one goal, e.g. sell surplus stock, promote a new item]
Keep each message short. Mix one offer, one reminder, and one value tip.
```

---

### Festival calendar prompt

```
List 5 upcoming Indian festivals or local occasions in the next 30 days that a [shop type] could use for a promotion.
For each one, suggest one simple offer idea.
Keep suggestions local and practical, not generic.
```

---

## Safety rules for all prompts

- Never paste customer names, phone numbers, or addresses into AI prompts.
- Do not ask AI to write fake reviews.
- Do not make medical, legal, or financial guarantee claims.
- Always read the AI reply before sending. Edit anything that sounds wrong.
- If the AI says something false or exaggerated, do not use that line.
