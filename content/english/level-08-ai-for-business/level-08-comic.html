Act 1

Ravi sees the competition getting ahead

🏪 Ravi · Hyderabad

Ravi's shop is doing okay. But the big mobile store two streets away put up a beautiful Diwali offer banner. WhatsApp groups are buzzing about their discounts. Ravi has better prices — but nobody knows.

"My prices are lower but they have a fancy poster and a proper message. I don't even know how to start writing something like that."
🏪 Ravi writes his first promo

Ravi opens his phone. He types a simple instruction into AI. He gets back a professional Diwali offer message — ready to paste into WhatsApp in 60 seconds.

Ravi's prompt
Write a short WhatsApp message for my mobile accessories shop in Hyderabad. We have 25% off on all phone cases and earphones this Diwali weekend. Keep it friendly and under 60 words.
🪔 Diwali Offer — Ravi Mobile Accessories! 🪔

Get 25% OFF on all phone cases and earphones this Diwali weekend! 📱✨
Saturday & Sunday only. Don't miss it!

📍 [Your shop address]
📞 [Your number]

Share with family & friends! 🙏
💡 Skill 1: Write Promotional Messages

Tell AI: your business name, what offer you have, the dates, and the tone (friendly / professional / urgent). AI writes the message. You paste it. Done.

Act 2

Meena turns her tiffin service into a real business

🍱 Meena · Nalgonda

Meena makes the best tiffin in the colony. Idli, sambar, upma, pesarattu — all fresh, all home-made. 30 regular customers. But when a housing society asked for her menu with prices and a tagline, she froze. She had never written one.

"How do I write a menu? I just cook. I don't know what to write or how to say it nicely."
🍱 Meena asks AI for her menu

She tells AI what she makes, her prices, and who she serves. In two minutes she has a full menu — with a tagline, item descriptions, and a contact section.

Meena's prompt
I run a home tiffin service in Nalgonda. I make idli (₹40), upma (₹35), pesarattu (₹45), and full meals on request (₹80). I deliver by 8am. Write a simple WhatsApp menu with a catchy tagline. Keep it warm and homely.
🍽️ Meena's Home Tiffins — "Fresh from kitchen, straight to your heart" 🍽️

• Idli + Sambar + Chutney — ₹40
• Upma + Chutney — ₹35
• Pesarattu + Ginger Chutney — ₹45
• Full Meal (on request) — ₹80

📦 Home delivery by 8am | Fresh daily | Hygienic kitchen
📞 [Contact Meena]
💡 Skill 2: Write Product & Service Descriptions

List your items, prices, and what makes you special. AI turns it into professional text you can share anywhere — WhatsApp groups, notice boards, colony announcements.

Act 3

Suresh stops dreading customer complaints

✂️ Suresh · Karimnagar

Suresh is a skilled tailor. But last week a customer sent a furious WhatsApp message — the shirt was 5 days late. Suresh got defensive, typed an angry reply, then deleted it. He sat staring at the screen for 20 minutes.

"I know I'm late but I can't say that rudely. And I can't sound weak. How do I reply?"
✂️ Suresh pastes the complaint into AI

He copies the customer's angry message and asks AI to write a polite, professional, apologetic reply. AI gives him a calm, respectful response in 30 seconds.

Suresh's prompt
My customer sent this message: "My shirt is still not ready! You said 3 days, it's been 5! This is very unprofessional!" Write a polite, apologetic, professional WhatsApp reply. I want to keep the customer. Do not sound rude or weak.
Dear [Customer name],

I sincerely apologise for the delay — you are absolutely right to be upset, and I understand your frustration. This is not the standard I hold myself to.

Your shirt will be ready by tomorrow evening. I will personally call you once it is done. As a gesture of apology, there will be no extra charge.

Thank you for your patience. I truly value your trust. 🙏
❌ Before AI

Suresh either sent an angry reply that lost the customer, or stayed silent and looked unprofessional.

✅ With AI

Calm, apologetic, professional reply in 30 seconds. Customer kept. Reputation protected.

💡 Skill 3: Reply to Customer Complaints

Paste the complaint. Tell AI: "Write a polite, professional reply. I want to keep the customer." AI handles the tone. You review and send.

Act 4

Divya captions 5 sarees in 3 minutes

🧣 Divya · Warangal

Divya resells Pochampally handloom sarees on Instagram. She spends 2 hours per post — taking photos, writing captions, finding hashtags, replying to DMs. With 20 sarees to post this week, she cannot keep up alone.

"I take the photo in 2 minutes. Then I spend 1 hour thinking of a caption. That's madness."
🧣 Divya describes each saree to AI

She describes the saree — colour, fabric, occasion — and asks AI for a ready-to-post caption with hashtags. Five sarees, five captions, 3 minutes total.

Divya's prompt
Write an Instagram caption for a Pochampally ikat silk saree. It's orange and gold with traditional geometric patterns. Perfect for Navratri and wedding functions. Price ₹3,200. Add 8 relevant hashtags. Keep it elegant and aspirational. Under 60 words.
Draped in heritage, woven in gold ✨

This Pochampally Ikat silk saree in radiant orange and gold is your perfect companion for Navratri and wedding festivities. Every thread tells a story 🧡

Price: ₹3,200 | DM to order

#PochampallySaree #IkatSilk #HandloomIndia #NavratriLook #WeddingFashion #TelanganaCraft #SareeLove #IndianWear
💡 Skill 4: Write Social Media Captions at Scale

Describe the product + occasion + price + tone. AI writes the caption with hashtags. Copy. Paste. Post. Repeat for every product in minutes, not hours.

Act 5

Ravi gets a free business advisor in his pocket

🏪 Ravi · The next level

Ravi's Diwali message worked. 12 new customers in two days. Now he wants a proper plan — not just one message but a full month of marketing. He asks AI to build one.

Ravi's planning prompt
I run a mobile accessories shop in Hyderabad. I want a 4-week WhatsApp marketing plan for November. Include weekly themes, message ideas, timing suggestions, and 2 festival offers (Diwali week 1, Children's Day week 2). Keep it practical for a small shop owner.
📅 November Marketing Plan — Ravi Mobile Accessories

Week 1 (Diwali): "Gift a phone case this Diwali!" — 25% off. Message Tuesday + Saturday 6pm.
Week 2 (Children's Day): "Best gift for kids — earphones & cases" — kids-friendly bundles. Message Thursday 5pm.
Week 3: New stock arrival — "Fresh Accessories Just In!" — Show 5 new products with photos.
Week 4: End of month — "Last chance November deals" — clearance offer on older stock.

💡 Always post at 6–8pm. Always include your phone number. Always end with a call to action.
🏪 Ravi's important reminder

Ravi reads the plan carefully. He adjusts it for his shop — changes the prices, adds his actual phone number, removes the suggestion that didn't fit. He uses AI as a starting point, not a final answer.

"AI gave me a plan in 1 minute. A consultant would charge ₹5,000 for this. But I still read every line and changed what didn't fit my shop."
💡 Skill 5: Plan Your Business Marketing

Ask AI for a marketing calendar, a festival offer plan, or a WhatsApp message schedule. Use it as a starting draft. Always review and adjust for your real situation. AI is a free advisor — not a guaranteed oracle.
