# mitraailife.com — Unified Platform Plan

> Purpose of this document: Record the new one-site strategy for Mitra AI Life. Instead of maintaining many separate websites, mitraailife.com becomes the single public platform with separate learning tracks behind clear top-banner buttons.

---

## 1. Strategic Decision

Use **one website**: `mitraailife.com`.

Do not create separate public websites for every idea right now. For a single-founder operation, one platform is easier to publish, advertise, maintain, explain, and improve.

The website should still keep every track separate. The rule is:

**One brand, one URL, many separate tracks.**

Do not mix all content into one course ladder. Each top-banner button opens a focused section with its own body design, tone, target learner, and content path.

---

## 2. Top Banner Navigation Order

Every major page should use the same top title/banner navigation in this exact order:

| Order | Button Label | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | `/` or `site/index.html` | Explain the full Mitra AI Life platform |
| 2 | AI for Daily Life | `/daily-life/` | Existing adult/family AI curriculum |
| 3 | AI for Students | `/students/` | School student AI literacy |
| 4 | AI Tuition | `/tuition/` | Subject tuition, starting with Hindi basics |
| 5 | Spoken English | `/spoken-english/` | Practical spoken English for Indian learners |
| 6 | AI Project Kits | `/projects/` | College/engineering project kits |
| 7 | AI for Small Business | `/small-business/` | Shopkeepers, freelancers, home businesses |
| 8 | AI for Teachers | `/teachers/` | Lesson plans, worksheets, classroom support |
| 9 | Contact | `/contact/` | WhatsApp, email, parent/institution enquiry |

Use these exact public labels unless the founder changes them later.

---

## 3. Home Page Role

The current landing page should become the new **Home** page.

Home should explain all services clearly, not only the current AI Daily Life course.

Recommended Home headline:

**Practical AI and learning skills for every Indian family**

Supporting line:

**Learn daily AI skills, school support, spoken English, small business growth, teacher tools, and college project kits — all in one trusted Mitra platform.**

Home page sections:

1. Hero: one-line platform promise
2. Track cards: the eight main service tracks
3. Short intro video: explain the full platform in 2 minutes
4. Why one family can use many tracks
5. Free-first promise for beginner content
6. Safety and human-review promise
7. Contact / WhatsApp enquiry

Home should not look like a generic corporate homepage. It should feel like a clear family education platform for India.

## 4. Track Positioning

### AI for Daily Life

This is the current Level 1-10 course.

It can include these topics inside the adult/family life track:
- AI for parents
- AI for senior citizens
- AI for women at home
- fake news and scam awareness
- deepfake awareness
- privacy and safe AI use
- household planning
- daily writing, budgeting, learning, travel, and communication

Route recommendation: `/daily-life/`

Existing content can remain under `content/english/` and `content/telugu/` while the site navigation presents it as the Daily Life track.

### AI for Students

School student AI literacy. This teaches students how to understand and use AI safely, not regular school subjects.

Tone: colorful, safe, age-appropriate.

Route recommendation: `/students/`

### AI Tuition

Subject tuition using AI-assisted content. First focus: elementary Hindi catch-up.

Tone: child-friendly, slow, repetitive, never shaming.

Route recommendation: `/tuition/`

First product: Hindi Foundation Sample Pack.

### Spoken English

Do not build generic language learning. Focus only on practical spoken English for Indian learners.

Tone: confidence-building, audio practice, real-life roleplay.

Route recommendation: `/spoken-english/`

### AI Project Kits

Premium college and engineering project kits.

Tone: professional, technical, clean, dark theme possible.

Route recommendation: `/projects/`

### AI for Small Business

Practical AI support for shopkeepers, freelancers, tiffin services, home businesses, service providers, and local entrepreneurs.

Tone: business-friendly, direct, results-first.

Route recommendation: `/small-business/`

### AI for Teachers

AI support for teachers to create lesson plans, worksheets, quizzes, explanations, and classroom activities.

Tone: respectful, practical, classroom-safe.

Route recommendation: `/teachers/`

## 5. Shared Layout, Different Body Magic

All pages should share:
- same top banner navigation
- same Mitra AI Life logo/brand
- same footer
- same analytics setup
- same contact route
- same basic accessibility and mobile behavior

Each track page can have a different body theme:

| Track | Body Theme Direction |
|---|---|
| Home | warm, trustworthy, family education platform |
| AI for Daily Life | simple, friendly, practical adult learning |
| AI for Students | bright, colorful, classroom-safe |
| AI Tuition | playful, large visuals, slow learning feel |
| Spoken English | confidence, voice, daily conversation scenes |
| AI Project Kits | professional dark engineering theme |
| AI for Small Business | energetic, local business, WhatsApp/Instagram examples |
| AI for Teachers | clean classroom, worksheets, organized planning |

This gives variety without creating separate maintenance systems.

---

## 7. Placeholder Page Rules

Each placeholder page should include:
- clear track title
- who it is for
- what problem it solves
- what will launch first
- whether it is free, paid, or premium
- a contact / interest button
- no fake finished courses

Do not overbuild placeholders. They should explain the track and collect interest while the Daily Life track is completed.

---

## 8. Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Homepage becomes confusing | Use clear cards and short labels |
| Too many tracks at once | Build placeholders only, finish one track at a time |
| Brand feels unfocused | Use one umbrella promise: AI and learning skills for every Indian family |
| Child safety risk | No public comments on child-facing tuition/student pages |
| Maintenance grows again | Shared header/footer/components and one deploy flow |
| Premium project support overload | Keep Project Kits clearly separate and paid |

---

## 9. Founder Rule

One site does not mean one product mixed together.

It means one trusted home with separate doors.

Build one door fully, then the next.
