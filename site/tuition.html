First launch focus

  • Lesson 1: अ, आ, इ, ई.
  • Lesson 2: क, ख, ग, घ.
  • Lesson 3: क plus matras intro.
  • Practice: worksheet and 10-question quiz.

Safety rule

This is practice support, not a school replacement. Child-facing pages should avoid public comments and avoid collecting sensitive child data.

Frequently asked questions

Which class level is AI Tuition for?

The first launch is elementary Hindi catch-up suitable for primary school children. More subjects will be added later.

Will this replace school?

No. AI Tuition is gentle practice support, not a school replacement. Children should keep attending school.

Do you collect personal data about my child?

No. Child-facing pages avoid public comments and avoid collecting sensitive child data.

How long is each lesson?

Each lesson is short, with slow pronunciation, simple writing practice, and a small quiz at the end.
