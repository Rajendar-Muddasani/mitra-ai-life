# AI for Small Business — Overview Video Script (English)

> Production use: read this aloud at a natural speaking pace. Target duration: 90–120 seconds.  
> Voice tone: warm, conversational, like a knowledgeable friend — not a corporate pitch.  
> Visual notes are in [square brackets] for the editor.

---

## Segment 1 — Hook (0:00 – 0:12)

[Visual: phone screen showing a flood of WhatsApp messages]

You run a business. Every day your phone is full — customer questions, complaints, people asking for prices, people who never replied.

Writing the right reply every single time takes time you do not have.

---

## Segment 2 — The problem (0:12 – 0:28)

[Visual: a shop owner staring at a blank message draft]

Most small business owners already know what they want to say. They just take too long to write it. Or the message comes out wrong — too formal, too casual, or just not quite right.

And when festival season comes, or a big sale is due, the promotion never gets written because there is always something more urgent.

---

## Segment 3 — What AI does here (0:28 – 0:52)

[Visual: typing a short prompt into ChatGPT on a phone, then a polished message appearing]

This is where AI helps. You describe what you want in plain words. AI gives you a draft in seconds. You read it, edit one or two words, and send.

No special training needed. If you can type a WhatsApp message, you can use these prompts.

---

## Segment 4 — What this pack covers (0:52 – 1:14)

[Visual: four card tiles appearing one by one — replies, offers, reviews, planning]

The Customer Replies and Promotions Starter Pack has four modules.

First: fast customer replies — for enquiries, delays, follow-ups.

Second: offer writing — for WhatsApp, Instagram, and poster text.

Third: review and complaint handling — so you stay calm under pressure.

Fourth: weekly and festival planning — so promotion never gets left to the last minute.

---

## Segment 5 — Who it is for (1:14 – 1:28)

[Visual: quick montage — kirana, tiffin box, salon, phone repair, tuition board]

This pack is for kirana stores, tiffin services, home bakers, salons, repair shops, tuition teachers, and anyone running a local business in India.

No technical background needed. Everything is in simple language.

---

## Segment 6 — Call to action (1:28 – 1:42)

[Visual: Mitra AI Life logo, contact email, Telugu button visible]

To get access or ask a question, click the button on this page. A Telugu version is also available from the top-right button.

Mitra AI Life — practical AI for the way you already work.

---

## Production notes

- Record in one clean take if possible. Pause naturally between segments.
- Background: quiet room, no reverb. Laptop fan off.
- Music: soft ambient background at -18dB under voice, fade out at end.
- If using TTS: Google Cloud TTS en-IN-Chirp3-HD-Charon or en-IN-Neural2-D at speaking rate 0.92.
- Poster frame: use frame at 0:28 (shop owner + phone, warm lighting).
