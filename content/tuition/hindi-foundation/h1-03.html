H1-03 · Swar Set 2

उ, ऊ, ए, ऐ

This lesson adds four more vowels and strengthens discrimination. The learner now hears short-versus-long patterns and begins comparing similar sounds without panic.

Lesson outcome

  • Recognize four more swar
  • Associate each sound with one familiar object or word
  • Prepare for a mixed sound check in H1-04

Repeat rhythm

उRepeat 7 times. Use उल्लू as anchor image.
ऊRepeat 7 times. Use ऊन as the sound anchor.
एRepeat 7 times. Use एड़ी or a simple echo word.
ऐRepeat 7 times. Use ऐनक as the memorable anchor.

Anchor cards

उ
उल्लू
A familiar owl picture helps the child remember the start sound.
ऊ
ऊन
Wool gives a clean longer sound anchor.
ए
एड़ी
The word can be taught visually with a heel picture or spoken model.
ऐ
ऐनक
Glasses make this sound easy to remember visually.

Practice

  1. Play one sound at a time and ask the learner to point to the correct letter card.
  2. Place उ and ऊ together. Ask which one sounds shorter.
  3. Place ए and ऐ together. Ask the learner to repeat after the teacher three times each.
  4. Trace all four letters once, then circle the favorite anchor picture.
  5. Finish with a mixed 8-sound recap from H1-02 and H1-03 together.

Quiz outline

Questions

  • Which letter matches glasses?
  • Which word is the anchor for ऊ?
  • Which sound is shorter: उ or ऊ?
  • Which card should be picked after hearing ए?
  • What happens after this lesson?

Answer key

  • 1. ऐ
  • 2. ऊन
  • 3. उ
  • 4. ए
  • 5. Mixed sound confidence check
