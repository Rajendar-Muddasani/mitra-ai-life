Frequently asked questions

Who are AI Project Kits for?

B.Tech, BCA, MCA, and diploma students who need a complete project kit with code, documentation, and a working demo.

Will you do the project for me?

No. Kits include guided code, documentation, and demo support so you learn while you build. We do not encourage academic shortcuts.

How is this different from the free content?

Project kits are a premium, higher-support track with full deployment guidance and one-to-one help. The free Daily Life lessons remain free.

Can I get help during my viva?

Yes. Premium support includes preparation help so you can confidently explain your project to faculty.
