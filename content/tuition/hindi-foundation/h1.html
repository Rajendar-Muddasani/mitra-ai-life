Letters and Sounds

Hindi has 12 vowel sounds called swar and 33 main consonants called vyanjan. These are the building blocks of every word. Listen, see, say, and trace — slowly and many times.

All 12 swar — स्वर

Each card shows one vowel with a real Hindi word and picture. Say the word aloud every time you see the card.

Watch and listen — synced swar video

Say along with Mitra. The video shows the letter, a realistic picture, the Hindi word, pronunciation, and English meaning while the voice says the same item.

All 33 vyanjan — व्यंजन

Hindi has 33 main consonants grouped by sound family (varga). The cards below show every consonant with its exact Devanagari shape and pronunciation guide.

Watch and listen — every vyanjan 7 times

The video shows each consonant on screen and says it 7 times slowly. Watch, listen, and say it along.
