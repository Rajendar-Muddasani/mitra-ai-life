# Level 1: First Step — Full Storyboard
**For:** Non-techie common man, India, Tier 2/3 cities
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes
**Format:** Interactive scrollable comic + future video/animation
**Language:** English (Telugu version follows same structure)
**Characters:** Ravi (shopkeeper, Hyderabad), Priya (homemaker, wife), Mitra (AI guide character — blue circle with friendly face), Rahul (student, Warangal)

---

## Timing Overview

| Segment | Name | Duration | Format | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook & Welcome | 5 min | Animated title + character intro | Feel excited, not scared |
| 2 | Meet the Characters | 4 min | Comic strip panels | Relate to the story |
| 3 | What Is AI? | 8 min | Illustrated explainer cards | Understand without jargon |
| 4 | Demo 1: Ravi's Letter | 10 min | Chat simulation + story | See the power |
| 5 | Demo 2: Priya's Recipe | 8 min | Chat simulation in Telugu | Telugu works too! |
| 6 | Demo 3: Rahul Studies | 6 min | Chat simulation | Students benefit too |
| 7 | Safety First | 8 min | Warning comic + rules | Trust but verify |
| 8 | Your First Try | 6 min | Interactive prompt box | One tiny action |
| 9 | Recap | 3 min | 4 summary cards | Lock in learnings |
| 10 | Level 2 Teaser | 2 min | Preview cards + CTA | Stay excited |

**Total: ~60 minutes**

## Segment 1 — Hook & Welcome (5 min)

### Scene 1.1 — Big Opening (30 sec)
**Visual:** Dark blue background. The word "AI" in huge letters, glowing. Then it morphs into the word "Helper."
**Audio/Caption:** "You have been hearing this word everywhere. News, TV, WhatsApp groups. But what IS it? Today — you find out."
**Animation:** Letters morph and glow. Warm, not scary.

### Scene 1.2 — The Promise (1 min)
**Visual:** Split screen. Left: Person struggling for 45 minutes to write something. Right: Same person done in 2 minutes using AI. Satisfaction face.
**Caption:** "By the end of this lesson, you will use AI to write a message in 30 seconds that would normally take you 10 minutes."
**Audio:** Upbeat background music (Indian classical fusion)

### Scene 1.3 — Who This Is For (1 min)
**Visual:** A row of illustrated people — shopkeeper, homemaker, student, auto driver, retired teacher, government worker.
**Caption one by one:** "This is for you... and you... and you."
**Message:** "No engineering degree needed. No special computer needed. Just your phone and curiosity."

### Scene 1.4 — Meet Mitra (2.5 min)
**Visual:** A friendly blue circle character "Mitra" bounces in from the side. Big smile. Wave.
**Mitra says:** "Namaste! I'm Mitra — your AI learning friend. I'll be with you through this entire lesson. Let's go!"
**Caption:** "Mitra = Friend. That's what AI can be for you. A helpful friend."
**Animation:** Mitra does a small dance. Stars appear. Fun and warm.

## Segment 2 — Meet the Characters (4 min)

### Scene 2.1 — Ravi's Introduction (1.5 min)
**Visual:** Comic panel. Exterior of a kirana shop in Hyderabad. Ravi behind the counter.
**Caption:** "Meet Ravi. He runs a small shop in L.B. Nagar. 39 years old. Has a smartphone. Never used AI."
**Show:** Ravi serving a customer, smiling. Happy in his world.

### Scene 2.2 — Ravi's Problem (1.5 min)
**Visual:** Ravi at home. Evening. Sitting at table with a blank paper and pen. Clock on wall shows 7:30 PM. His son Arjun is doing homework nearby.
**Ravi thinks (thought bubble):** "I need to write this letter to the school... but how do I start? Should it be in English? Telugu? What is the formal format?"
**Show:** Ravi crumples paper and throws it. 3 crumpled papers on floor already.
**Caption:** "45 minutes. 3 failed drafts. Ravi is frustrated."

### Scene 2.3 — Priya's Suggestion (1 min)
**Visual:** Priya enters the room. She's been on WhatsApp.
**Priya says:** "Ravi, my friend Sunitha used something called ChatGPT to write her Diwali greetings. It was beautiful! Why don't you try?"
**Ravi's face:** Sceptical. Curious. One eyebrow raised.
**Caption:** "Ravi doesn't believe it. But he has nothing to lose."

## Segment 3 — What Is AI? (8 min)

### Scene 3.1 — The Library Analogy (3 min)
**Visual:** Animated sequence. A huge library. Billions of books. A little robot reading all of them rapidly (speed lines, books flying).
**Caption:** "Imagine a helper who has read billions of books, articles, and conversations. Every topic. Every language."
**Then:** The helper looks at you, ready to answer.
**Caption:** "That helper is AI. It learned from all that reading — and now you can ask it anything."

### Scene 3.2 — How You Talk to It (2 min)
**Visual:** Person typing on phone. Simple sentences like "Help me write a message" or "Explain this to me simply."
**Caption:** "You type what you need — in normal words. Like texting a friend. No commands. No technical words."
**Show:** AI gives back helpful text. Fast.
**Caption:** "AI replies. You read it. You use what is correct."

### Scene 3.3 — What AI Is NOT (3 min)
**Visual:** Three "myth busting" panels.
**Panel 1:** Shows scary robot from Hollywood movies → BIG RED X → Shows friendly Mitra character. Caption: "Not a scary robot."
**Panel 2:** Shows magic wand → BIG RED X → Shows typing chat window. Caption: "Not magic. It is a software program."
**Panel 3:** Shows AI making a big decision for a family → BIG RED X → Shows human looking at AI output and deciding. Caption: "Not a decision-maker. You decide. AI suggests."

## Segment 4 — Demo 1: Ravi's Letter (10 min)

### Scene 4.1 — Opening ChatGPT (2 min)
**Visual:** Ravi's phone screen. He opens ChatGPT for the first time. The interface appears. Clean.
**Mitra narrates:** "Ravi opens ChatGPT. Free app. Works on Android, iPhone, and web browser."
**Show:** The text cursor blinking. Empty chat window.
**Caption:** "Ravi doesn't know what to type. So he types exactly what he would say to a helpful friend."

### Scene 4.2 — Ravi Types His Prompt (2 min)
**Visual:** Ravi's fingers on keyboard (close-up). He types slowly but surely.
**Text appearing on screen (animated, letter by letter):**
> "Write a formal letter to my son's school principal. My son Arjun, Class 5B, missed 3 days of school because of fever. I want to apologise and request that he be allowed to write the missed tests."
**Ravi hits Send.**
**Suspense moment:** 2-second pause. Dots appear (AI thinking)...

### Scene 4.3 — AI Responds (3 min)
**Visual:** Letter appears on screen, line by line — like it's being typed in real-time.
**Show the full letter on screen.**
**Ravi's face:** Eyes go wide. Jaw drops a little. He leans closer to the screen.
**Thought bubble:** "This is... exactly what I needed? In 30 seconds?"

### Scene 4.4 — Ravi Edits and Sends (3 min)
**Visual:** Ravi reads the letter. He changes [Your Name] to his name. Changes [date] to the correct dates.
**Caption:** "Always personalise! Replace the bracketed parts. Make it your own."
**Ravi copies it, pastes into a WhatsApp message to his wife first.**
**Priya reads it, nods with approval.**
**Ravi's face:** Huge smile. Relief.
**Caption:** "45 minutes → 3 minutes. Problem solved. That is AI."

## Segment 7 — Safety First (8 min)

### Scene 7.1 — The Funny Wrong Answer (3 min)
**Visual:** Comic strip. Ravi asks AI who the current Chief Minister of Telangana is.
**AI gives a name that was correct 2 years ago — but wrong now.**
**Ravi is confused. He checks his phone news app. Different name!**
**Mitra appears:** "AI's information has a cut-off date. It does not know recent news automatically. Always verify current events on a news site."
**Caption:** "AI is not Google. It does not update in real-time."

### Scene 7.2 — 4 Safety Rules (5 min)
**Visual:** Four illustrated rule cards appear one by one with sound effects.

**Rule 1 (Red):** 🚫 "Never share your Aadhaar, bank account number, or passwords with AI."
**Illustration:** Person typing Aadhaar number → Big red X appears.
**Caption:** "AI is not your bank. Treat it like a public space. Do not share private information."

**Rule 2 (Amber):** 🩺 "Medical advice from AI must be verified by a real doctor."
**Illustration:** AI giving medicine dosage → Doctor checking it.
**Caption:** "AI can suggest ideas. A doctor can confirm what is right for your body."

**Rule 3 (Blue):** 📰 "For current news, dates, and events — check a news site."
**Illustration:** AI giving old information → News app showing correct current info.

**Rule 4 (Green):** ✅ "Always read AI output before using it."
**Illustration:** Person reading letter from AI → Editing one word → Sending.
**Caption:** "AI is a first draft, not a final answer. You are the editor."

## Production Notes

### Illustration style
- Cartoon/comic style — not realistic. Think: simple shapes, bold outlines, bright colours.
- Characters are expressive — big eyes, clear emotions (frustrated, surprised, happy, relieved)
- Indian setting: kirana shop, home kitchen, school hallway, Hyderabad streets (autorickshaws, kolam, etc.)
- Warm colour palette for story panels. Dark blue for tech/demo sections.

### Audio (for video version)
- Background: Indian classical + light electronic fusion (calm, upbeat)
- Voice narration: Warm Telugu-accented English — like a knowledgeable neighbourhood uncle/aunty
- Character voices: Distinct. Ravi = low, relaxed. Priya = warm, cheerful. Mitra = clear, friendly.
- Sound effects: Notification sounds, paper crumple, typing keys, "ding!" for correct answers

### Accessibility
- All key text is available in both English and Telugu
- Font size minimum 16px for all body text
- High contrast in all sections
- No time-critical interactions (no timers or forced pacing)
