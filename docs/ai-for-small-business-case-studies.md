# AI for Small Business — Illustrative Case Study Examples

> These are realistic illustrative examples based on the 6 featured business categories. They show how a typical business owner could use the prompts and templates from the first pack. These are NOT real testimonials — label them clearly as "Example" or "How it works" on any public page.

---

## Example 1 — Kirana store, Hyderabad

**Business:** Neighbourhood kirana and grocery store, 1 owner, WhatsApp number on shop board.

**Challenge before:** Answering the same stock questions all day — "Do you have Bru 200g?" "What time do you close?" Manually typing each reply was cutting into the day.

**What they used:**
- Enquiry reply prompt to create a standard stock-check reply template
- Business-hours auto-reply to handle off-hours messages

**What changed:**
- Reply time dropped from 10–15 minutes per message to 2 minutes
- The owner copies a saved template, edits the product name, and sends
- Weekly offer message now takes one prompt instead of staring at a blank screen for 20 minutes

**Example message produced:**
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out to Sri Lakshmi Stores. Yes, we have Bru 200g in stock. Price: ₹89. Open Mon–Sat 7 AM to 9 PM. Come in or reply here to reserve. 🙏

## Example 2 — Tiffin service, Bengaluru

**Business:** Home tiffin subscription service, 1 person, 40 daily customers, orders via WhatsApp.

**Challenge before:** Monthly subscription renewals were being done one message at a time. Festival closures caused confusion and complaints. Delay apologies were either too cold or too long.

**What they used:**
- Order confirmation template for new and renewed subscriptions
- Delay apology template for days when cooking ran late
- Festival promotion prompt for Ugadi special menu

**What changed:**
- Subscription renewal now uses one message with the customer's name inserted — takes 30 seconds per customer
- Festival promotion for Ugadi got 12 advance orders from a single WhatsApp broadcast

**Example message produced:**
> Hi Suresh! 😊 Your tiffin subscription has been renewed for June. Delivery starts Monday 7:30 AM. Please keep your tiffin box ready. For any changes, reply here. Thank you for choosing Annapurna Tiffins!

## Example 3 — Salon, Vijayawada

**Business:** Women's beauty parlour, 2 chairs, appointment-based, Google Business profile active.

**Challenge before:** Negative Google reviews felt personal and the owner did not know how to respond without sounding defensive. Instagram captions were skipped because writing felt too slow.

**What they used:**
- Negative review reply template (thank, acknowledge, offer resolution)
- Instagram caption prompt for a new bridal package

**What changed:**
- One negative review about wait time got a calm, professional reply in 3 minutes instead of being ignored for 3 days
- Bridal package caption generated in one prompt, posted same day, got 4 enquiries

**Example message produced (Google review reply):**
> Thank you for taking the time to share this. We're sorry the wait felt longer than expected on that visit — we have since adjusted our scheduling. We'd love to make your next visit better. Please reach out at [number] and we'll make sure you're taken care of. — Priya, Glamour Studio

## Example 4 — Home bakery, Chennai

**Business:** Home baker, custom cakes and Diwali snacks, Instagram-active, orders via DM and WhatsApp.

**Challenge before:** Diwali order rush was chaotic — no standard message for confirming custom orders, no promotion went out until it was too late.

**What they used:**
- Festival promotion prompt (Diwali snack boxes, early-order discount)
- Custom order confirmation template

**What changed:**
- Diwali promotion WhatsApp broadcast was sent 10 days early
- All custom orders now get a confirmation with order details and pickup date — no "did you get my order?" follow-up messages

**Example message produced (Diwali broadcast):**
> 🪔 Happy Diwali from Sweet Corner! 🪔 Book your Diwali snack box before 10 November and get ₹50 off. Boxes start at ₹299 — murukku, mixture, ladoo, and more. Limited boxes available. Reply YES or call [number] to book yours. Made fresh at home. 🙏

## Example 5 — Tuition centre, Hyderabad

**Business:** Class 8–10 tuition, 1 teacher, 35 students, parent WhatsApp group and individual messages.

**Challenge before:** Parents asked the same questions repeatedly — exam schedule, holiday dates, fee dues. Individual replies to 35 parents was exhausting. Fee reminders felt awkward to write.

**What they used:**
- Business-hours and schedule reply template
- Fee reminder template (polite, non-pressuring)
- New batch promotion prompt for summer admissions

**What changed:**
- Holiday schedule update was sent as a single broadcast instead of 35 individual messages
- Fee reminder template is now a fixed message the teacher only needs to personalise with the amount and month

**Example message produced (fee reminder):**
> Hi [Parent name], hope Rahul is doing well! This is a gentle reminder that the tuition fee of ₹1,200 for June is due. Please pay at your earliest convenience. Accepting GPay, PhonePe, and cash. Thank you! — Sri Vidya Tuitions

## Example 6 — Mobile phone repair shop, Vizag

**Business:** Phone repair, 1 technician, walk-in and doorstep pickup, Google Business profile.

**Challenge before:** Customers called repeatedly asking "is my phone ready?" Repair status updates were verbal only. No review replies — 3 unanswered negative reviews sat on the profile.

**What they used:**
- Job status update template (phone ready for pickup)
- Positive review reply template
- Negative review reply template for one complaint about repair time

**What changed:**
- Proactive status update message now goes when a repair is done — calls asking "is it ready?" dropped
- All 3 Google review replies written and posted within one afternoon using templates

**Example message produced (repair ready):**
> Hi! Your phone is ready for pickup at TechFix Vizag. Repair: screen replacement. Amount: ₹1,100. Open today till 8 PM. Please bring this message for fast handover. Thank you for trusting us! 🙏
