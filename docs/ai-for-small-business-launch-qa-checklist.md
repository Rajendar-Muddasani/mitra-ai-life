# AI for Small Business — Launch QA Checklist

> Purpose: a quick review checklist to run before any Small Business page, pack, prompt, or template is made public. Use this to catch pricing confusion, claim safety issues, mobile readability problems, and language quality gaps.

---

## Section A — Pricing clarity

- [ ] Every page is clear about what is free and what is paid.
- [ ] No paid offer is mentioned without a visible price, or a clear statement that pricing is sent on enquiry.
- [ ] No upsell language appears before the learner has understood the free content.
- [ ] Combo discounts or bundles show the breakdown clearly.
- [ ] The payment policy page is linked and up to date before any paid offering goes live.

---

## Section B — Claim safety

- [ ] No message claims guaranteed business growth, sales, or income.
- [ ] No message claims guaranteed marks, placement, or admission.
- [ ] No message includes fake urgency language (e.g. "only 2 spots left" without being true).
- [ ] No message includes medical, legal, or financial advice without a qualified expert review.
- [ ] No prompt or template instructs the user to write fake reviews or false testimonials.
- [ ] Every AI-generated sample is labelled or described as an example, not as a guaranteed output.
- [ ] Over-automation warnings are present where AI-only workflows could harm customer relationships.

---

## Section C — Mobile readability

- [ ] Every page loads on a mid-range Android phone without layout overflow.
- [ ] Video poster images display correctly before the user presses play.
- [ ] The hero section is readable on a 5-inch screen without zooming.
- [ ] Buttons are at least 44px tall and have visible tap targets.
- [ ] No horizontal scroll appears on small screens.
- [ ] Text size is at least 0.88rem for body copy and 1rem for list items.
- [ ] The top-right Telugu button is visible without needing to scroll the nav.

---

## Section D — English language quality

- [ ] All English copy uses simple sentences (under 25 words each wherever possible).
- [ ] No jargon without explanation: terms like AI, prompt, chatbot are introduced plainly.
- [ ] Example businesses are Indian and recognisable — not Silicon Valley or US-only.
- [ ] No copy implies that AI does all the work — the human must review before sending.
- [ ] FAQs answer questions a real shop owner would actually ask.
- [ ] Error or safety messages avoid blame language.

---

## Section E — Telugu language quality

- [ ] Telugu copy is natural conversational Telugu, not machine-translated.
- [ ] Telugu example businesses use Telangana/Andhra Pradesh references.
- [ ] Technical terms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Business, AI) remain in English.
- [ ] Festival examples use locally relevant festivals: Ugadi, Sankranti, Dasara, Diwali, Eid.
- [ ] Telugu copy has been reviewed by a native speaker before going live.
- [ ] The Telugu page links correctly to Telugu legal pages (privacy-te.html, payment-policy-te.html).

---

## Section F — Enquiry and lead capture

- [ ] The contact or enquiry path on the Small Business page is working.
- [ ] The enquiry email subject line pre-fills with Small Business context.
- [ ] There is at least one visible CTA above the fold on both English and Telugu pages.
- [ ] The "Send Your Business Idea" bottom CTA is working and the email address is correct.
- [ ] No form collects private customer data that is not needed.

---

## Section G — Content completeness

- [ ] The English module pack definition exists.
- [ ] The Telugu module pack definition exists.
- [ ] The prompt packs file is ready for learner use.
- [ ] The template samples file is ready for learner use.
- [ ] The 6 featured business categories are listed on the landing page.
- [ ] An overview video exists for both English and Telugu landings.
- [ ] The landing page "What learners get" section matches the actual pack contents.

---

## How to use this checklist

Before publishing any new Small Business content:
1. Run through sections A, B, and C for every new page.
2. Run section D for every English content file.
3. Run section E for every Telugu content file.
4. Run section F before enabling any payment or enquiry flow.
5. Run section G before calling any phase of the track publicly "ready".

Mark each item as checked only after personally verifying it — not just assuming it passes.
