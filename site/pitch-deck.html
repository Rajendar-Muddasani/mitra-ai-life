Risks & Mitigations

Honest risk assessment.
Every risk has a plan.

Risk Why it's real How we mitigate it
AI tools change fast ChatGPT, Gemini interfaces change constantly — our lesson demos may go stale Lessons teach concepts + prompting skill, not UI clicks. Updatable in hours with our Python pipeline.
Low willingness to pay Indian edtech has trained users to expect free content Permanent free levels build trust. ₹100 price point is lower than a textbook. Combo deals create anchor.
Big-brand competition Google, NASSCOM, Coursera enter AI literacy for India We win on local language depth and daily-life framing. No global brand will build a Telugu-first AI course about writing WhatsApp messages to vegetable suppliers.
AI accuracy & safety AI outputs can be wrong. Learners may over-trust AI advice on health, legal, financial matters Every level includes explicit safety caveats. Content policy prohibits medical/legal advice as AI output. Human review on every lesson before publish.
Founder single-point risk Currently a one-person operation Content pipeline is AI-assisted and scripted — any hire can follow it. Hiring a Telugu content editor is the first team expansion.
