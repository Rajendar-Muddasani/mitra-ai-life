# Level 9: AI for Income

## Who this is for
Anyone who wants to earn money using AI skills — freelancers, fresh graduates,
homemakers, part-time workers, and small business owners who want to add a new income stream.
No coding required. Works from a phone or basic laptop.

## Lesson objective
Show learners how to use AI to perform freelance services, create content for pay,
and find real income opportunities — starting with zero investment.

## Theme
Teal + Emerald green (`#0d9488`, `#10b981`)

## Duration
3–4 hours

## Price
₹700 (0 INR early offer)

## Characters

### Kavya
- 24-year-old from Warangal, recently finished B.Com
- No job yet. Has a phone, basic English, and Level 1–8 skills
- Wants to earn ₹5,000–₹10,000 a month from home

### Srinivas
- 38-year-old civil engineer from Hyderabad, working full-time
- Wants a side income on weekends — uses AI to write LinkedIn posts for local businesses
- Earns ₹2,000–₹4,000 extra per month in his spare time

### Padma
- 45-year-old homemaker from Vijayawada
- Excellent cook and Hindi/Telugu speaker
- Uses AI to create recipe content and YouTube video scripts for her new channel

### Raju
- 27-year-old from Nizamabad, runs a small photo studio
- Uses AI to write captions, edit client WhatsApp messages, and offer "AI content packages" to clients

## Story arc (5 acts)

### Act 1 — The Idea: AI Skills Have Real Market Value
Kavya realises her AI prompt skills from Levels 1–8 are services people pay for.
She sees three types of income opportunities: freelance writing, social media content, and AI-assisted services.
Lesson: What you learned in this course is already a marketable skill.

### Act 2 — Kavya's First Freelance Gig
Kavya finds a local saree shop owner who needs product descriptions and Instagram captions.
She uses AI to write 10 product descriptions and 5 captions in 45 minutes.
She charges ₹500 for the work. The client loves it. She gets a repeat order.
Lesson: How to pitch, price, and deliver simple AI writing services.

### Act 3 — Srinivas's Weekend Side Income
Srinivas helps 3 local businesses (a dentist, a restaurant, a tutoring centre)
with weekly LinkedIn and WhatsApp content — 2 posts each per week.
He charges ₹1,500/month per client. 3 clients = ₹4,500/month, 4–6 hours of work.
Lesson: Retainer-based content creation with AI is a sustainable side income.

### Act 4 — Padma Creates a Content Business
Padma starts a Telugu food YouTube channel. She uses AI to write video scripts,
titles, descriptions, and captions — in Telugu. She publishes 2 videos a week.
After 3 months she gets 1,000 subscribers and monetises.
Lesson: AI lowers the barrier to content creation — you supply the personality and knowledge.

### Act 5 — Raju Adds an "AI Package" to His Studio
Raju offers existing photo studio clients a ₹200 add-on: AI-written captions,
product descriptions, and a WhatsApp promotional message for every photoshoot.
His revenue per client goes up by 15–20% with almost no extra time.
Lesson: Add AI services as a premium layer on top of what you already do.

---

## 5 skills taught

1. **Identify and pitch AI freelance services** — what to offer, how to explain it, how to price it
2. **Deliver AI writing work** — product descriptions, social posts, email newsletters
3. **Build a content creation workflow** — scripts, titles, captions, translations
4. **Price your time correctly** — per-piece pricing, monthly retainers, value-based pricing
5. **Use AI to scale without burning out** — templates, batch work, reusable prompts

## Quiz questions (5 questions, pass 4/5)

1. Kavya wants to offer AI writing services to local businesses. What is the best first step?
   - a) Build a professional website first
   - b) Identify one nearby business that needs content and offer to do a small trial for free or low cost
   - c) Wait until she has 100 hours of AI experience
   - d) Ask AI to find clients for her automatically
   → Answer: b

2. Srinivas charges ₹1,500/month per client for 2 social media posts per week.
   He has 3 clients. How many hours per week does he need if each post takes 20 minutes with AI?
   - a) 8 hours
   - b) 12 hours
   - c) 2 hours
   - d) 20 minutes
   → Answer: c  (6 posts × 20 min = 120 min = 2 hours per week)

3. Padma wants to start a Telugu food YouTube channel. How does AI most help her?
   - a) AI cooks the food for her
   - b) AI writes her video scripts, titles, and descriptions so she can focus on cooking and presenting
   - c) AI automatically uploads and edits her videos
   - d) AI finds sponsors for her channel
   → Answer: b

4. Raju adds an AI captions package to his photo studio. What is the smartest pricing approach?
   - a) Give it free to all clients
   - b) Charge the same as a full photography session
   - c) Offer it as a small add-on (₹200–₹300) that adds clear value to the client's photoshoot
   - d) Refuse to charge because AI did the work
   → Answer: c

5. What is the most important thing to remember when charging for AI-assisted work?
   - a) Never tell the client you used AI
   - b) Your value is in choosing the right prompts, editing the output, understanding the client's needs — not just pressing a button
   - c) AI work should always be free
   - d) Only experts can charge for AI work
   → Answer: b

## Ethics note (always included)
- Be transparent with clients that you use AI as a tool (you don't need to hide it)
- Always review and edit AI output before delivering — you are responsible for quality
- Don't promise results you can't guarantee (e.g. "this will go viral")
- Pricing should reflect your effort and value, not deceive the client
