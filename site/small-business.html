🏪 Ravi · Mobile Shop · Hyderabad

Writing a WhatsApp promotional message

Ravi noticed a competitor down the street offering festival discounts. He wanted to send a WhatsApp message to his customers but didn't know how to write one that felt genuine.

PROMPT YOU CAN COPY

I run a mobile repair and accessories shop in Hyderabad. Write a short WhatsApp message for Dussehra. Offer 15% off on screen guards and phone covers until Oct 15. Sound genuine and friendly, not like a mass forward.

WHAT AI GAVE BACK

🎉 Dussehra Mubarak! This festive season, protect your phone with our premium screen guards and covers — now at 15% off till Oct 15. Visit Ravi Mobile Shop, Hyderabad. Tell your friends! 📱

🍱 Meena · Tiffin Service · Nalgonda

Writing a WhatsApp menu

Meena makes fresh tiffin every morning — idli, upma, pesarattu. A housing society asked for her menu. She had never written one and didn't know where to start.

PROMPT YOU CAN COPY

I run a home tiffin service in Nalgonda. I make idli (₹40), upma (₹35), pesarattu (₹45), and full meals on request (₹80). I deliver by 8am. Write a simple WhatsApp menu with a catchy tagline. Keep it warm and homely.

WHAT AI GAVE BACK

🍽️ Meena's Morning Tiffins — Fresh, Hot, Delivered by 8am

Idli · ₹40 | Upma · ₹35 | Pesarattu · ₹45 | Full Meals · ₹80

📞 Order by 7pm for next-day delivery. Made with love, every morning.

✂️ Suresh · Tailor · Karimnagar

Replying to a customer complaint

A customer said their shirt came back with a wrong measurement. Suresh felt embarrassed but didn't know how to apologise professionally without losing the customer.

PROMPT YOU CAN COPY

I am a tailor in Karimnagar. A customer complained that the shirt I stitched has wrong measurements. Write a polite WhatsApp reply that apologises sincerely, offers a free correction, and asks them to come back. Keep it short and warm.

WHAT AI GAVE BACK

Dear [Name], I'm truly sorry about the measurement issue. That should never have happened. Please bring the shirt back at your convenience — I will correct it for free, no questions asked. Thank you for your patience. 🙏

🧣 Divya · Saree Reseller · Warangal

Writing captions for 5 sarees in minutes

Divya sells sarees on WhatsApp and Instagram. Writing a caption for each saree was taking 20 minutes. She needed a faster way to describe 5 sarees at once.

PROMPT YOU CAN COPY

I sell sarees on WhatsApp and Instagram. Write 5 short product captions for these sarees: 1) Pink Banarasi silk ₹2400, 2) Blue Kanjivaram cotton ₹1800, 3) Yellow pochampally ikat ₹1200, 4) Green chanderi ₹900, 5) Red georgette ₹1500. Each caption: 2 lines, emoji, price, "DM to order".

Frequently asked questions

I only know WhatsApp. Can I still use this?

Yes. The first launch focuses on WhatsApp and Instagram workflows that you already know.

Do I need to write good English?

No. AI helps you write customer messages, captions, and offers in simple language. You stay in control of the final words.

Will this bring me more customers?

AI helps with promotions and replies. Real growth still depends on your service quality and consistency.

Is my customer data safe?

Do not paste private customer details into AI tools. We teach you which information is safe to share and which is not.
