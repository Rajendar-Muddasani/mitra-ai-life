Frequently asked questions

Will AI write my homework for me?

No. We teach students how to use AI for understanding ideas, not for copying answers. Honest study habits matter more than shortcuts.

Is this safe for school children?

Yes. The track follows school-safe practices and teaches privacy, integrity, and how to spot misinformation.

Should parents be involved?

We recommend parents review what their child uses, especially for younger students. Lessons include parent guidance.

What ages is this for?

First launch focus is upper-primary and secondary students who already use a phone or shared family device.
