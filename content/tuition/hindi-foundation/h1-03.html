Practice

  1. Play one sound at a time and ask the learner to point to the correct letter card.
  2. Place उ and ऊ together. Ask which one sounds shorter.
  3. Place ए and ऐ together. Ask the learner to repeat after the teacher three times each.
  4. Trace all four letters once, then circle the favorite anchor picture.
  5. Finish with a mixed 8-sound recap from the earlier lessons together.
