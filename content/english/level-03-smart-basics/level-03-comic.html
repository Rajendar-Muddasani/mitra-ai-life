Skill 1 of 5

Check before you trust.

AI can sound very confident even when it's wrong. It does not know what it doesn't know. Your job is to be the fact-checker.

👩
Meena · Teacher, Vizag
She asked AI: "What is the exam fee for AP Board Class 10?"
AI replied: "The fee is ₹450."
She told 30 students this. The actual fee had changed to ₹600. Parents were upset.

The 3-Check Rule — always do this for important facts

  • 🔍
    Check 1 — Ask AI when it last knew this. Add to your prompt: "Is this information likely to be current?" AI will often warn you if the data could be outdated.
  • 🔗
    Check 2 — Ask AI where to verify. Prompt: "What official website should I check to confirm this?" Then actually go there.
  • 🧑‍⚕️
    Check 3 — For health, legal, money matters — always consult a human expert. AI is a starting point, never the final word on these topics.
BETTER PROMPT — Meena should have written:
"What is the exam fee for AP Board Class 10? Note: I need current information. Is this likely to be accurate today? Where can I verify this on an official website?"
AI RESPONSE (safer version):
"Based on my training data the fee was approximately ₹450, but exam fees are updated regularly. I strongly recommend verifying the current fee on the official BSEAP website at bse.ap.gov.in before sharing this information."
⚠️ NEVER BLINDLY TRUST AI FOR:
Medicine dosages · Legal deadlines · Government fees or dates · Financial figures · Medical diagnoses · Court judgments
Skill 2 of 5

Ask AI to walk you through it,
one step at a time.

When you get a long AI answer all at once, it can feel overwhelming. Step-by-step mode turns a wall of text into a clear guide you can actually follow.

🎓
Rahul · Engineering Student, Guntur
Rahul asked AI to explain how to apply for a college scholarship. AI gave him a 400-word answer. He read it, felt confused, and gave up.

His friend said: "Try asking it to break it into numbered steps."
❌

Without step-by-step

AI dumps everything at once. Hard to follow. You miss steps. You feel overwhelmed and stop.

✅

With step-by-step

AI gives you numbered steps, one at a time. You complete each step. You feel confident. You finish.

Step-by-step magic phrases — add these to any prompt

Magic Phrase 1
"Please explain this as numbered steps. Keep each step short."
Magic Phrase 2
"Break this into steps. After each step, pause and ask me if I am ready for the next one."
Magic Phrase 3 — for learning something new
"Explain this to me like I am a complete beginner. Use simple numbered steps and a real-life example from India."
RAHUL'S NEW PROMPT:
"How do I apply for a scholarship at NIT Warangal? Give me the steps as a numbered list. Keep each step under 2 sentences. I am a first-year student."
AI RESPONSE:
1. Visit the official NIT Warangal website and navigate to Student → Scholarships.
2. Check the eligibility criteria — most scholarships require you to maintain a CGPA of 7.0 or above.
3. Download and fill the scholarship application form from the portal.
4. Attach required documents: admission letter, income certificate, bank passbook copy.
5. Submit the form at the Student Affairs Office before the deadline shown on the portal.
⚠️ Verify exact dates and documents on the official website — they change each year.
Skill 3 of 5

Same message.
Different tone.

A good AI answer in the wrong tone can still cause problems. A complaint letter that sounds too aggressive. A professional email that sounds too casual. AI can rewrite anything in any tone you need — you just have to ask.

👩‍💼
Priya · HR Executive, Hyderabad
Priya needed to tell a job applicant they were not selected. She asked AI to write it. The first response sounded cold and dismissive. She used one prompt to fix it.
"Rewrite this to sound warm, respectful, and encouraging."

The Tone Toolkit — common tone requests that work

SituationAdd this to your prompt
Complaint letter"Rewrite this to sound firm but respectful, not angry."
Professional email"Rewrite this to sound formal and courteous for a manager."
Message to a friend"Rewrite this to sound warm, casual, and friendly."
Request to a stranger"Rewrite this to sound polite and humble."
Motivating a student"Rewrite this to sound encouraging and positive."
WhatsApp to vendor"Rewrite this to sound direct but not rude."
PRIYA'S ORIGINAL AI DRAFT (too cold):
"We regret to inform you that your application has been rejected. We received many applications and yours did not meet our requirements."
PRIYA'S FIX PROMPT:
"Rewrite this rejection message to sound warm, respectful, and encouraging. Keep it short. The candidate should feel valued even though they are not selected."
REWRITTEN VERSION:
"Thank you so much for taking the time to apply and interview with us. After careful consideration, we have moved forward with another candidate whose profile closely matched our current needs. This does not reflect on your potential — we encourage you to keep applying and wish you the very best in your career."
Skill 4 of 5

Ask twice.
Compare, then decide.

The first answer AI gives is not always its best. Asking two different ways — or asking for a second version — often reveals a much better answer. Smart AI users do this automatically.

🏪
Kiran · Shop Owner, Vijayawada
Kiran needed to choose between two mobile phones for his shop's payment system. He asked AI once and got a recommendation. Then he asked a second time with different framing — and got completely different reasoning. By comparing both, he made a smarter purchase decision.
1️⃣

Ask once — normal way

Get your first answer. Read it. Don't accept it immediately.

2️⃣

Ask again — different angle

Reframe the question from another angle, or explicitly ask: "Give me a second opinion on this."

⚖️

Compare and decide

Look at both answers. Where do they agree? Where do they differ? That difference is where your judgment matters most.

KIRAN'S FIRST PROMPT:
"Which Android phone is best for a small shop owner in India who needs to run PhonePe, WhatsApp Business, and a simple billing app? Budget ₹12,000."
KIRAN'S SECOND PROMPT (different angle):
"I want to buy a phone under ₹12,000 for my shop. My priority is battery life and reliability, not camera. Suggest options and tell me what to avoid."
WHAT KIRAN LEARNED BY COMPARING:
The first answer focused on features. The second highlighted battery and reliability — which was actually his real priority. He picked a phone mentioned in both answers. Best decision he made all month.
Power Phrase — ask for a second opinion
"Give me a second opinion on this. What might someone argue against your first answer?"
This forces AI to give you the other side — often more valuable than the first answer.
Skill 5 of 5

Build your personal
prompt library.

Every time you write a prompt that works really well, save it. Treat it like a recipe. Next time you need something similar, you just swap the details. This turns AI from a one-time helper into a permanent life tool.

📋
Smart habit — what advanced AI users do
They keep a simple Notes app (or WhatsApp Saved Messages) with their favourite prompts. They label each one. When they need it, they copy-paste and change the specifics. Takes 10 seconds. Saves 20 minutes.

5 prompt templates to save right now

Template 1 — Verify any fact
"Is the following information accurate? [paste the fact]. If you are not sure, say so and tell me where to verify it officially."
Template 2 — Step-by-step guide
"Explain how to [task] in clear numbered steps. I am a beginner. Use a simple Indian example."
Template 3 — Tone rewrite
"Rewrite this message to sound [tone: formal / friendly / firm / encouraging]: [paste your message]"
Template 4 — Second opinion
"You just told me [paste the answer]. Now give me a second opinion. What are the risks or downsides I should know?"
Template 5 — Daily decision helper
"Help me decide between [option A] and [option B] for [my situation]. Give me pros and cons as a simple table."

💡 How to save: Copy any template above → paste into WhatsApp Saved Messages, iPhone Notes, or Google Keep → label it with a short name. That's your prompt library started.

Practice Time

Try these yourself today.

Open ChatGPT or Gemini and do each one. You will be surprised how much better your results get.

Practice 1

Fact-check something

Ask AI about any government fee, exam date, or scheme. Then add "Is this current? Where can I verify this officially?" Compare what it says before and after.

Practice 2

Get step-by-step help

Pick any task you want to learn — cooking, applying for a scheme, setting up a mobile feature. Ask AI to explain it in numbered steps for a beginner.

Practice 3

Change the tone

Write a message to someone — a complaint, an apology, a request. Ask AI to rewrite it in two different tones. See which one fits better.

Practice 4

Ask for a second opinion

Next time AI recommends something — a product, a plan, a route — ask it: "Now give me the reasons why this might NOT be the best choice." Notice what it says.

Practice 5

Start your prompt library

Copy the 5 templates from Section 7 into your Notes app or WhatsApp Saved Messages right now. Give each one a short label. Your library is started.
