H1-02 · Swar Set 1

अ, आ, इ, ई

This lesson introduces the first four swar with a strict rhythm: see the letter, hear it many times, connect it to an image, then trace and say it yourself.

Lesson outcome

  • Recognize four early swar visually
  • Hear the difference between short and long forms
  • Use image anchors to remember sound and word together

Repeat rhythm

अSay 7 times. Short sound.
आSay 7 times. Long sound with आम.
इSay 7 times. Use इमली as anchor.
ईSay 7 times. Use ईख as anchor.

Anchor cards

अ
अनार
Pomegranate can support recall, but the focus is still the short sound.
आ
आम
Mango is bright, familiar, and memorable.
इ
इमली
Tamarind gives a clear beginning sound.
ई
ईख
Sugarcane supports the longer vowel shape.

Practice

  1. Watch each letter appear one by one in full-screen mode.
  2. Repeat after the teacher without hurry.
  3. Point to the matching picture after hearing the sound.
  4. Trace each letter two times.
  5. Mix the cards and ask: show me आ, show me इ, show me ई.

Quiz outline

Questions

  • Which letter matches mango?
  • Which letter matches tamarind?
  • Which sound is longer: इ or ई?
  • Which letter should be shown after hearing sugarcane?
  • What should the learner do before tracing?

Answer key

  • 1. आ
  • 2. इ
  • 3. ई
  • 4. ई
  • 5. Listen and repeat first
