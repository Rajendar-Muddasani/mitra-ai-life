1 Skill 1 — Write professional emails in 60 seconds

Deepa's story

The email that took 40 minutes

Deepa is an executive at a logistics company in Hyderabad. Every morning she writes 5 to 8 emails — to suppliers, clients, and her manager. It takes her almost an hour. Her manager noticed she was slow. Deepa was embarrassed.

👩‍💼 Deepa · Hyderabad

Deepa spends 40 minutes writing one email to a supplier asking about a delayed shipment. She rewrites it three times. It still sounds too harsh. She sends it and worries about the reply.

"I just need to ask where my order is. Why is this so hard to phrase politely?"
💡 The AI approach

A friend shows Deepa how to use AI for email drafting. She types a simple instruction and gets a polished, professional email in under 10 seconds.

Deepa's prompt

Write a polite professional email to a supplier. Our shipment order #4521 was due on 1 May 2026. It has not arrived yet. Ask them for an update, mention the delay is affecting our customers, and request a confirmed delivery date. Keep the tone firm but respectful.

✅ The result

AI gives Deepa a full professional email in 8 seconds. She reads it, changes one small detail, and sends it. Her manager notices the improvement and asks what changed.

Subject: Follow-Up on Delayed Shipment — Order #4521

Dear [Supplier Name], I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to follow up on order #4521, which was expected by 1 May 2026 but has not yet been received. The delay is beginning to affect our customer commitments, and we would appreciate your urgent attention. Could you please confirm the current status and provide a revised delivery date? Thank you for your prompt response...

💡 The email prompt formula

Who you are writing to + What the issue is + What you want them to do + Tone (polite / firm / friendly)

  • 1Tell AI who you are writing to
  • 2Describe the situation in 2 or 3 bullet points
  • 3Say what you want the reader to do
  • 4Mention the tone — polite, urgent, friendly, firm
  • 5Read the output, edit names and details, then send

❌ Without AI

Deepa writes one email in 40 minutes. It sounds too harsh. She rewrites it 3 times and still feels unsure. Her inbox has 7 more emails to go.

✅ With AI

Deepa writes a polished email in 60 seconds. She handles all 8 emails in under 15 minutes. She feels confident and professional all morning.

2 Skill 2 — Summarise meetings and write reports instantly

Sunitha's story

The meeting no one remembered

Sunitha is an HR manager in Guntur. After every team meeting she has to write a summary and send it to her director. It takes her 45 minutes per meeting. Some days she has three meetings. She often forgets small details and her director asks questions she cannot answer.

👩‍💼 Sunitha · Guntur

After a 1-hour team meeting about leave policy changes, Sunitha opens her notebook and tries to reconstruct everything that was discussed. Her notes are scattered. She misses two important decisions.

"I was talking so much in the meeting I forgot to write things down properly."
💡 The AI approach

Sunitha learns to jot down quick bullet points during the meeting — not full sentences. Then she pastes them into AI and asks for a formatted summary. The whole thing takes 3 minutes.

Sunitha's prompt

Here are my rough notes from today's HR team meeting. Please write a clear meeting summary with: 1. Date and attendees section 2. Key points discussed 3. Decisions made 4. Action items with owner names 5. Next meeting date if mentioned My notes: - leave policy update discussed, 12 days CL confirmed - Raju asked about carry forward — decided max 5 days - new joining process to be updated in handbook by May 15 - next meeting June 3 - attended by Sunitha, Raju, Kavitha, Prasad

✅ The result

AI formats Sunitha's rough notes into a clean professional meeting summary. Her director is impressed. Sunitha now shares summaries within 5 minutes of every meeting ending.

Meeting Summary — HR Team | Date: 6 May 2026
Attendees: Sunitha, Raju, Kavitha, Prasad

Key Points: Leave policy review completed. Casual Leave confirmed at 12 days per year. Carry-forward capped at 5 days maximum.

Decisions Made: Joining process documentation to be updated in employee handbook by 15 May 2026.

Action Items: Kavitha — update handbook. Raju — circulate revised policy email.

Next Meeting: 3 June 2026.

📋 Meeting summary prompt formula

Give AI your rough notes (even messy ones) + ask for sections: Discussion Points, Decisions, Action Items, Next Steps. AI will do the formatting.

  • 1During the meeting, jot short bullet points — no full sentences needed
  • 2After the meeting, paste your notes into AI
  • 3Ask AI to format it into a professional summary with sections
  • 4Read once, add any names or dates that were missed
  • 5Send within 5 minutes of the meeting ending — impress everyone
3 Skill 3 — Improve your resume in 10 minutes

Anand's story

The resume that never got replies

Anand is a fresh B.Tech graduate from Nellore. He has been applying for software testing jobs for two months. He has sent his resume to 30 companies. Only one replied. His college placement officer told him his resume was weak but didn't explain how to fix it.

👨‍💻 Anand · Nellore

Anand's resume has "Responsible for testing" and "Good communication skills" written multiple times. It looks like every other resume. HR managers skip it in under 10 seconds.

"I have real skills but I don't know how to write them down properly. My resume looks boring."
💡 The AI approach

Anand pastes one section of his resume into AI and asks for specific improvements. AI rewrites his bullet points to sound stronger and more specific, using action verbs and numbers.

Anand's prompt

I am a fresh B.Tech graduate applying for software testing roles. Here is my current experience section from my resume: "Responsible for testing web applications. Worked in a team. Good communication with developers. Found bugs and reported them." Please rewrite these bullet points to sound stronger. Use action verbs, be specific, and add numbers where possible. Keep it to 4 bullet points, each under 20 words.

✅ The result

AI rewrites Anand's weak bullet points into strong, specific, ATS-friendly lines. After updating his resume, he gets 4 interview calls in the next 2 weeks.

• Executed 200+ manual test cases across 3 web application modules, reducing defect leakage by 30%
• Collaborated with 5-member development team to resolve 45 critical bugs within sprint deadlines
• Documented detailed bug reports in JIRA, improving developer fix-time by 2 days per sprint
• Performed regression testing across Chrome, Firefox, and Edge ensuring cross-browser compatibility

📝 Resume improvement tip

Always give AI your current bullet points and ask it to improve them — do not ask AI to write a resume from scratch. AI-only resumes sound generic. Your real experience + AI polish = strong resume.

❌ Weak resume bullet

"Responsible for testing web applications. Good communication skills."

✅ Strong resume bullet

"Executed 200+ manual test cases across 3 web modules, reducing defect leakage by 30%"

  • 1Paste ONE section of your resume into AI (experience, skills, or summary)
  • 2Tell AI your target job title and experience level
  • 3Ask for action verbs, specific numbers, and concise format
  • 4Review carefully — add real numbers only you know
  • 5Repeat for each section of the resume
4 Skill 4 — Practise interviews with AI as your mock interviewer

Anand's next step

Preparing for the interview — alone

Anand gets an interview call. Now he needs to prepare. He has no one to practise with at home. His coaching centre is too expensive. His friend group has no one who works in tech yet. AI becomes his mock interviewer — available any time, patient, and specific to his role.

👨‍💻 Anand · Nellore

Anand has never had a real interview before. He does not know what questions to expect or how to answer the "tell me about yourself" question without rambling for 5 minutes.

"I know my subject but I freeze when someone asks me to explain myself. I need to practise."
💡 The AI approach

Anand asks AI to act as a hiring manager for a software testing role. He practises answering 10 common questions. AI gives him feedback after each answer — what was good, what to improve, what to add.

Anand's prompt

Act as a hiring manager for a software testing role at a mid-sized tech company. Interview me for this role. I am a fresh B.Tech graduate with 6 months of internship experience in manual testing. Ask me one question at a time. After I answer, give me brief feedback. Start with: "Tell me about yourself."

✅ The result

After 3 practice sessions with AI, Anand's answers become focused and confident. He knows exactly how to open with his intro, how to answer behavioural questions, and how to end with a strong close.

AI Feedback: "Good opening. Your intro covered your degree and internship clearly. For improvement: mention one specific project by name — interviewers remember stories more than general statements. Try: 'During my internship at [company], I tested a payment module that had 200+ test cases...' This is much stronger."

🎯 Interview practice tips

After each answer, ask AI: "What was weak in my answer and how should I improve it?" — this is more valuable than asking AI to just list questions.

  • 1Tell AI your target role, company type, and your experience level
  • 2Ask AI to act as the hiring manager and interview you
  • 3Answer each question in full — type it as you would say it
  • 4Ask AI for feedback on your answer before moving to the next question
  • 5Do this 3 times — your answers will get significantly stronger
5 Skill 5 — Build a study plan and create presentation outlines

Vikram's story

The exam that was 3 weeks away

Vikram is a third-year engineering student in Tirupati. He has 6 subjects to study, university exams in 3 weeks, and a project presentation due next Friday. He does not know where to start. He spends 2 hours every evening deciding what to study instead of actually studying.

👨‍🎓 Vikram · Tirupati

Vikram has 6 subjects and no idea how to divide his time. He opens his computer to study and ends up on YouTube. He feels guilty but does not know how to organise himself.

"I have so much to study but I don't know how to split my time. I just feel stuck."
💡 The AI approach — Study Plan

Vikram tells AI his subjects, exam date, and how many hours per day he can study. AI gives him a complete week-by-week study plan with daily targets. It even tells him which subjects need more time based on their typical difficulty.

Vikram's prompt

I am a 3rd year electronics engineering student. I have 6 subjects: Digital Signal Processing, VLSI Design, Embedded Systems, Microprocessors, Control Systems, and Communication Theory. My exams start on 28 May 2026. Today is 6 May 2026. I can study 4 hours per day on weekdays, 6 hours on weekends. Create a week-by-week study plan with daily targets. Prioritise harder subjects and include one day of revision per week.

💡 AI approach — Presentation Outline

For his project presentation, Vikram tells AI his project topic and asks for a 10-slide outline. AI gives him a clear structure with slide titles and what to include in each slide. Vikram builds his presentation in 90 minutes instead of an entire weekend.

Vikram's prompt

Create a 10-slide presentation outline for my final year project: "Smart Irrigation System using IoT Sensors and Arduino" Include: Problem statement, existing solutions, our solution, components used, circuit diagram slide, working explanation, test results, cost, conclusion, references. Each slide: title + 3 to 4 bullet points of what to include.

✅ The result

Vikram follows his AI-generated study plan. He covers all subjects systematically and completes his presentation with 3 days to spare. He scores better than in any previous semester.

Week 1 (May 6–12): Focus on DSP and Control Systems — 4 hrs/day. Day 7: Full revision of Week 1 material.
Week 2 (May 13–19): VLSI Design and Communication Theory — 4 hrs/day. Day 14: Full revision.
Week 3 (May 20–27): Embedded Systems + Microprocessors — 4 hrs/day. Days 26–27: Full mock tests all subjects.
Tip: Start with DSP — it has the highest overlap with VLSI and earns you double benefit early.

📚 Study plan tip

Always tell AI your actual available hours, not ideal hours. A realistic plan you follow beats a perfect plan you abandon in 3 days.
