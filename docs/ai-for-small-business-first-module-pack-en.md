# AI for Small Business — First English Module Pack

> Purpose: define the first English module pack for the Small Business track so content, prompt packs, templates, and short videos can be built from one source.

## Pack name

Customer Replies and Promotions Starter Pack

## Who this is for

- shop owners who already use WhatsApp with customers
- home bakers, tiffin services, and snack sellers
- salon, repair, clinic, and appointment-based service businesses
- tuition teachers and coaching centres that need better parent communication
- freelancers and local service providers who want faster day-to-day messaging

## What problem it solves

Many small business owners know their work well but lose time on repetitive customer replies, last-minute offer writing, review handling, and simple promotion planning.

This pack gives them a practical starting system instead of generic AI theory.

## Simple promise

Use AI to reply faster, write clearer offers, handle reviews better, and plan simple promotions without sounding robotic.

## Track fit

- track: AI for Small Business
- launch language: English first
- Telugu follow-up: planned as the next module-pack adaptation
- commercial fit: free preview plus enquiry-led working pack at first

## What the learner should be able to do after this pack

- reply to common customer messages in a calm and clear tone
- write WhatsApp and Instagram offer text faster
- handle positive and negative review replies without panic
- build a simple weekly or festival promotion plan
- avoid unsafe use of private customer data in AI tools

## Pack structure

### Module 1: Fast customer replies

Goal: help a business answer enquiries, follow-ups, delays, and stock questions quickly.

Lessons:
- how to turn rough customer intent into a clear reply
- how to keep replies short, polite, and useful
- how to create variants for busy hours, closed hours, and delay situations

Outputs:
- enquiry reply prompt set
- follow-up message templates
- delay and apology templates
- business-hours reply examples

### Module 2: Offers and promotions

Goal: help a business write offers that are easy to understand and locally relevant.

Lessons:
- how to write a one-line offer without hype
- how to adapt the same offer for WhatsApp, Instagram, and poster copy
- how to create festival and weekend variations

Outputs:
- offer-writing prompt set
- caption templates
- poster headline examples
- seasonal promotion examples

### Module 3: Reviews and complaint handling

Goal: help a business respond to praise, complaints, and recovery situations with better tone.

Lessons:
- when to thank, when to clarify, and when to move a complaint offline
- how to avoid rude or defensive wording
- how to write short response ladders for common review types

Outputs:
- positive review reply prompts
- negative review recovery templates
- complaint-escalation response examples
- tone guardrails list

### Module 4: Weekly and festival planning

Goal: help a business turn random promotion activity into a simple repeatable calendar.

Lessons:
- how to plan 1 week of messages with one business goal
- how to map offers to weekends, local events, and festivals
- how to reuse one idea across multiple short messages

Outputs:
- weekly planning worksheet
- festival promotion prompt set
- 7-day message calendar sample
- repeat-campaign checklist

## Tone and writing rules

- use simple English only
- prefer Indian small-business examples over startup jargon
- keep prompts short enough for phone users
- avoid exaggerated business-growth claims
- keep the learner in control of the final message

## Safety boundaries

- do not paste private customer records into AI tools
- do not create false medical, legal, or refund promises
- do not generate fake reviews or misleading claims
- do not present AI output as guaranteed business advice
