# AI for Small Business — First 6 Business Categories

> Purpose: decide which business types to feature publicly on the Small Business track landing page, in examples, and in the first module pack.

## Decision

The first 6 public business categories are:

| # | Category | Why this first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kirana and retail stores | Highest volume in India, WhatsApp-native, easy to demonstrate |
| 2 | Tiffin and food delivery | Very common in Telangana/Andhra, clear daily use case |
| 3 | Salons and beauty parlours | Appointment-based, review-sensitive, promotion-heavy |
| 4 | Home bakers and snack sellers | Growing segment, Instagram-active, offer-writing need is clear |
| 5 | Tuition and coaching centres | Parent communication is a major daily burden |
| 6 | Repair and service shops | Phone repair, appliance repair, plumbing, electrical — common and under-served |

## Category detail

### 1 — Kirana and retail stores

Primary pain points:
- stock enquiries on WhatsApp all day
- no time to write offers or seasonal promotions
- difficulty getting or responding to Google reviews

AI use cases:
- quick stock reply template
- weekly offer message
- Google review reply
- WhatsApp broadcast message

Example businesses: neighbourhood kirana in Hyderabad, medical store in Vijayawada, stationery shop in Tirupati

---

### 2 — Tiffin and food delivery

Primary pain points:
- subscription confirmations and renewals
- delivery delay apologies
- menu and pricing updates to many customers at once

AI use cases:
- monthly subscription confirmation
- delay apology message
- menu update WhatsApp broadcast
- festival special promotion

Example businesses: office tiffin service in Hyderabad, home kitchen subscription in Bengaluru, school lunch delivery in Vizag

---

### 3 — Salons and beauty parlours

Primary pain points:
- appointment reminders and rebooking
- handling cancellations politely
- Instagram caption writing
- customer feedback and review replies

AI use cases:
- appointment reminder template
- rebooking follow-up
- Instagram caption for a new service or offer
- reply to a Google review

Example businesses: ladies beauty parlour in Hyderabad, unisex salon in Vijayawada, bridal makeup home service

---

### 4 — Home bakers and snack sellers

Primary pain points:
- custom order confirmation and pricing communication
- Instagram content for products
- festival offer promotions
- handling bulk order enquiries

AI use cases:
- custom order confirmation
- Instagram product caption
- festival pre-order promotion
- bulk order enquiry reply

Example businesses: home bakery in Hyderabad, custom cake maker in Bengaluru, Diwali snack seller

---

### 5 — Tuition and coaching centres

Primary pain points:
- parent communication about fees, holidays, and results
- WhatsApp broadcast for admissions
- responding to complaints about teaching or schedules
- promotion of free trials or new batches

AI use cases:
- admission enquiry reply
- fee reminder message
- parent update about holiday schedule
- new batch promotion

Example businesses: class 6–10 tuition in Hyderabad, competitive exam coaching in Vijayawada, spoken English classes in Nellore

---

### 6 — Repair and service shops

Primary pain points:
- device/job status update messages
- repair delay apologies
- review replies for trust building
- WhatsApp quote messages

AI use cases:
- job status update message
- delay apology
- Google review reply after successful repair
- quick quote message

Example businesses: mobile phone repair shop in Hyderabad, laptop service centre in Vijayawada, home appliance repair, AC service

---

## How to use these categories on the public page

- show these as the first 6 audience cards or icons on the Small Business landing page
- use one example from each category in the prompt pack and template samples
- build the first video around one or two of these to make the overview concrete
