# Level 1: First Step

## Lesson objective
Help a complete beginner understand that AI is a practical helper for daily tasks.

## Lesson flow
1. What AI means in simple words
2. What AI can do today
3. What AI cannot be trusted to do blindly
4. One example prompt for a family task
5. One example prompt for a student task
6. One safe practice challenge

## Suggested assets
- 1 story page
- 1 cartoon strip
- 1 comic explainer
- 1 short video under 2 minutes
- 1 worksheet
