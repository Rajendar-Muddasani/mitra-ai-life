What launches first

Spoken English stays focused on confidence in common situations, not grammar-heavy theory or generic language-app content.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know grammar first?

No. We start with confidence in common situations, not grammar theory. You learn by speaking real sentences.

Will I get speaking practice?

Yes. Each module uses listen, repeat, roleplay, and real-life scenarios so the words become natural.

Is this useful for job interviews?

Yes. Practice modules include basic interview answers for students and job seekers.

What if I feel shy?

You can practice quietly with the AI before speaking with a real person. Confidence builds step by step.
