H1-01 · Start From Zero

Hindi Is For Me

This first lesson removes fear. The learner hears that Hindi can be learned slowly, by listening first. We introduce only two sounds today: अ and आ.

Watch Lesson Video See Repeat Plan Open Asset Manifest

Lesson outcome

  • The learner feels safe to restart Hindi
  • The learner hears the difference between अ and आ
  • The learner connects आ with आम as a memorable anchor

Lesson flow:

No-shame welcome, अ and आ repetition, contrast practice, and first tracing cue.

Listen rhythm

अRepeat 7 times slowly. Pause. Repeat 3 times together.
आRepeat 7 times slowly. Pause. Repeat with the word आम.

Anchor cards

अ
अ
Short open sound. Mouth opens lightly.
आ
आम
Longer sound with mango as the first anchor image.

Practice

  1. Say अ after the teacher voice 7 times.
  2. Say आ after the teacher voice 7 times.
  3. Point to the mango image and say आम slowly 3 times.
  4. Trace one big अ and one big आ on paper.
  5. Ask the learner: Which sound feels longer, अ or आ?

Quiz outline

Questions

  • Which sound is short: अ or आ?
  • Which sound is used in आम?
  • Which letter should the learner trace after hearing the longer sound?
  • What picture anchor is used for आ?
  • What should happen first in this lesson: listening or testing?

Answer key

  • 1. अ
  • 2. आ
  • 3. आ
  • 4. Mango / आम
  • 5. Listening first
