# Business Name Options

## Final recommendation

### 1. Mitra AI Life Education
- meaning: a friendly AI companion brand focused on practical learning for daily life
- why it works: warm, Indian, memorable, and clearer for an education-first launch
- brand strength: it feels helpful without sounding too technical, while still telling people this is a learning platform
- best use: public-facing brand for the website, pitch deck, and first learning products

## Naming structure recommendation

### Public brand
- use: `Mitra AI Life Education`
- why: simple for learners, clean on the website, and easy to speak in English and Telugu

### Workspace folder
- use: `Mitra_AI_Life`
- why: clear, readable, and already aligned with the current local setup

### Best repo names
- first choice: `mitra-ai-life`
- second choice: `mitra-ai-life-academy`
- third choice: `mitra-ai-life-platform`

### Repo name to avoid
- avoid: `mitra-of-ai-for-life`
- reason: too long, less natural to say, and harder to remember or share

### Future legal company name options
- chosen future legal name: `Mitra AI Life Education Private Limited`
- backup legal option: `Mitra AI Life Learning Private Limited`
- backup legal option: `Mitra AI Life Technologies Private Limited`

## Brand interpretation guidance

The phrase `Mitra AI Life Education` is already strong on its own. The longer expansion `Mother India training academy of AI for Life` can stay as an internal founder story, not the public expansion of the brand. Public users should see a simple meaning such as:

- `Mitra AI Life Education: practical AI help for daily life`
- `Mitra AI Life Education: learn AI for everyday use`
- `Mitra AI Life Education: simple AI learning for India`

## Other backup options if you want a second round

### 2. AI Mitra Life
- suggested repo: `ai-mitra-life`

### 3. Mitra Life Academy
- suggested repo: `mitra-life-academy`

## Decision note

If the goal is one clean brand you can use now and still grow later, the best current stack is:

- company brand: `Mitra AI Life Education`
- workspace: `Mitra_AI_Life`
- repo: `mitra-ai-life`
- website title: `Mitra AI Life Education`
