Frequently asked questions

Who are AI Project Kits for?

B.Tech, BCA, MCA, diploma students, final-year teams, and freshers who need a serious project with a real explanation path.

Will you do the project for me?

No. The track is guided learning and guided build support. It is not positioned as fake academic submission help.

What makes this different from the student track?

The student track teaches AI literacy and pre-college foundations. Project Kits are premium, higher-support builds focused on thesis, portfolio, viva, and job-readiness outcomes.

What should every serious kit include?

A project brief, milestone plan, architecture, starter code, final code path, deployment guide, report template, PPT template, viva questions, and known limitations.
