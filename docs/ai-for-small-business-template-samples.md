# AI for Small Business — Ready Template Samples

> Purpose: editable message templates for WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Business, and day-to-day customer communication. Replace [bracket text] with real details before sending.

---

## WhatsApp message templates

### New customer enquiry reply

```
Hi [Customer name or "there"],
Thank you for reaching out to [Business name]!

[Answer their question in 1–2 lines.]

To place an order or know more, reply here or call [number].

— [Your name], [Business name]
```

---

### Order confirmation

```
Hi [Customer name],
Your order has been confirmed! 🎉

Order: [describe what was ordered]
Delivery/pickup: [date or time]
Amount: ₹[amount]

If you have questions, just reply to this message.

Thank you for choosing [Business name]! 🙏
```

---

### Delay apology

```
Hi [Customer name],
We're sorry for the delay with your order.

Reason: [short explanation — stock issue, extra orders, etc.]
New expected date: [date]

We appreciate your patience. As a thank you, [add a small gesture if any — discount, extra item, etc.].

Apologies again. — [Business name]
```

---

### Follow-up (no response in 2 days)

```
Hi [Customer name],

[Repeat the key offer or question in 1 line.]

Let us know if you have any questions. Happy to help!

— [Business name]
```

```
Hope to see you again soon! — [Business name]
```

---

### Reply to a 1–2 star review

```
Thank you for letting us know about your experience.

We're sorry it didn't meet your expectations. [Acknowledge the specific issue briefly without argument.]

We'd love a chance to make it right. Please reach out to us at [number or email] so we can help.

— [Business name]
```

## Safety rules for all templates

- Review every message before sending. Edit anything that sounds robotic or wrong.
- Do not paste customer's private details (phone number, address) in AI-generated messages.
- Remove or edit any line that sounds like a medical, legal, or financial guarantee.
- Do not claim things you cannot deliver — refund promises, exact delivery times, guaranteed results.
- Festival messages: check the festival date before sending. Sending a Happy Diwali message on the wrong day looks careless.
