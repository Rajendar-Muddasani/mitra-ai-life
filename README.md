# AI Daily Life Academy

A planning-first workspace for a startup that teaches practical AI usage to everyday people in India.

## Current scope

This repository currently contains:
- phasewise business and product planning
- course level definitions, durations, and pricing
- English-first and Telugu-next content guidance
- a presentation-style HTML strategy page
- Copilot workspace instructions, prompts, skills, and agent definitions

## Working assumptions

- English is the source language for all lessons.
- Telugu is the first translated language.
- The first two learning levels remain free to maximize adoption.
- Payments start only after the product shows early traction.
- School and college content can exist under the same umbrella brand, but engineering project offerings are a separate premium track.
- AI generates the first draft of every asset, but a human reviews for clarity, safety, and factual accuracy before publishing.
