Quiz outline

Questions

  • Play a sound and choose the matching letter.
  • Play आम and choose the matching swar.
  • Which two sounds need the learner's extra revision?
  • Should the learner be pushed forward if still confused?
  • What is the next section after this?

Answer guidance

  • 1. Depends on the played sound card
  • 2. आ
  • 3. The missed sounds only
  • 4. No. Repeat calmly instead
  • 5. Barakhadi and consonant families

Move on rule

If a learner can correctly identify at least 6 of the 8 swar without stress, they can move to the next section. If they cannot, repeat the two earlier lessons. The goal is secure listening, not forced progress.
