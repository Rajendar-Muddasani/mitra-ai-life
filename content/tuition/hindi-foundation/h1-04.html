First Sound Confidence Check

This lesson checks listening confidence without pressure. The learner hears mixed swar from H1-02 and H1-03, matches sound to letter, and ends H1 ready for consonants.

Lesson outcome

  • Mix and recognize the first eight swar taught in the sample pack
  • Complete a parent-guided sound check calmly
  • Know whether to repeat H1 or move to H2

Confidence check flow

Round 1

Hear the sound. Point to the right card. No writing yet.

Round 2

Hear the anchor word. Pick the matching swar.

Round 3

Trace only the letters the learner misses.

Round 4

Celebrate the sounds that are already easy. Repeat only what is weak.

Quiz outline

Questions

  • Play a sound and choose the matching letter.
  • Play आम and choose the matching swar.
  • Which two sounds need the learner's extra revision?
  • Should the learner be pushed forward if still confused?
  • What is the next pack after H1?

Answer guidance

  • 1. Depends on the played sound card
  • 2. आ
  • 3. The missed sounds only
  • 4. No. Repeat calmly instead
  • 5. H2 consonants and letter families

Move on rule

If a learner can correctly identify at least 6 of the 8 swar without stress, they can start H2. If they cannot, repeat H1-02 and H1-03. The goal is secure listening, not forced progress.
