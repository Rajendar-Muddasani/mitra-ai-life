Grammar Basics · व्याकरण की शुरुआत

Grammar should feel like pattern practice, not a rule test. Start with one useful pattern, say many real sentences, then write a few examples after the sound feels natural.

How to use this page

Read each example aloud. Ask the child to repeat it, then point to the object at home or in the school bag. Write only after the child can say the sentence without pressure.

Lesson 1 — का / की / के

These words often mean "of" or "belonging to". The choice depends on the thing we are talking about.

का

Use before one masculine thing.

राम का बैग Ram's bag

की

Use before one feminine thing.

सीता की किताब Sita's book

के

Use before plural things.

बच्चों के खिलौने children's toys

Say these at home

कापापा का फोनfather's phone
काभैया का जूताbrother's shoe
कीमम्मी की चाबीmother's key
कीदीदी की कॉपीsister's notebook
केभैया के जूतेbrother's shoes
केबच्चों के रंगchildren's colours

Fix the sentence

Say the full sentence with the correct word. Then write the answer in a notebook.

राम ___ घर

Answer: का

रीना ___ किताब

Answer: की

बच्चों ___ खिलौने

Answer: के

मम्मी ___ चाबी

Answer: की

पापा ___ बैग

Answer: का

दोस्तों ___ नाम

Answer: के

Parent check

  • Use real objects: phone, bag, book, key, shoes, and toys are enough for the first week.
  • Say the correct sentence last: if the child says the wrong word, repeat only the corrected sentence.
  • Do not over-explain gender: use many examples first. The rule will feel natural after repetition.
  • Keep writing small: two corrected sentences in the notebook are enough.
