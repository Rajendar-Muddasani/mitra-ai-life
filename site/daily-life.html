Frequently asked questions

Is the AI for Daily Life course really free?

Yes. Levels 1 to 10 of AI for Daily Life are free to use. Any future paid support will be shown separately.

Do I need a laptop or computer?

No. Any smartphone with internet works. Lessons are designed mobile-first with simple visuals.

My English is weak. Can I still learn?

Yes. Lessons are written in very simple English, and Telugu versions are rolling out level by level.

Do I need any AI account to start?

You can read the lessons without any account. To try the AI tools shown in lessons, a free ChatGPT or Gemini account is enough.
