Example conversation
🎒
Can you explain what a food chain is in simple words?
🤖
Sure! A food chain shows who eats who in nature. It starts with a plant (producer), then a small animal eats the plant, then a bigger animal eats that small animal. Example: Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake. Energy moves step by step along the chain. Try drawing one for your school garden!
🎒
Can you just write my full assignment on ecosystems?
🤖
I can help you understand ecosystems really well so you can write it yourself! That way you actually learn it and your work is truly yours. Want me to break it into simple points you can expand?

Mitra Study Helper always encourages understanding over copying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will AI write my homework for me?

No. Mitra Study Helper explains, simplifies, and quizzes — but always asks you to write the final answer yourself. Academic honesty is built into every response.

Is this safe for younger children?

Yes. Class 6 and 7 content is designed for ages 10–12. No sign-up is needed for these classes. All content avoids adult topics. Parent notes are included.

Do students need coding knowledge to start?

No. Class 6 and 7 have zero coding. Python starts gently in Class 8 with fully guided notebooks. No prior experience is needed.

From which class do notebooks start?

A tiny intro appears in Class 7. The first real notebook is in Class 8. Notebooks become stronger in Classes 9 to 12 with guided, step-by-step instructions.

Does the chatbot know school subjects?

Mitra Study Helper can explain general concepts and simplify text from any subject. For Class 8 and above, it can also guide Python notebook steps.

Does it work for CBSE, ICSE, and SSC students?

Yes. The curriculum is board-neutral, covering AI awareness and Python in a way that broadly aligns with CBSE, ICSE, Cambridge, and SSC patterns without being locked to any one board.
