What You Will Learn in Class 8

Class 7 taught you to use AI tools well. Class 8 takes you one level deeper — you will understand why AI works the way it does, what its limits are, and how the same thinking skills can help you build your own ideas using AI.

🤖
How AI Actually Learns
AI vs ML vs Deep Learning. What training data is. Why AI gets things wrong — at a deeper level.
📊
Data — The Fuel of AI
What a dataset looks like. Clean vs messy data. Patterns, labels, and how AI finds meaning.
💬
How Language Models Think
What tokens are. Why GPT and similar models predict the next word. Why they can sound confident but be wrong.
⚖️
AI Ethics and Fairness
Where bias comes from in AI. What fairness means in practice. Big questions: jobs, privacy, deepfakes, who decides.
🇮🇳
AI Innovation in India
Real Indian AI projects in healthcare, farming, education, language, and space research.
🐍
Your First Python Code
Variables, print, lists, simple if — your very first steps in the language used to build most of today's AI.

What You Need to Start

Recommended: Complete Class 7 AI Daily Life Academy (or have equivalent experience using AI tools for school work). Class 8 builds directly on the prompting, critical thinking, and safety skills from Class 7.
  • A device with internet access (phone or computer)
  • A free AI tool account (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or similar)
  • A notebook for writing down prompts and observations
  • For Lesson 11 (Python): free access to Google Colab or Replit — no installation needed
  • Curiosity and willingness to ask "but WHY does AI do that?"
