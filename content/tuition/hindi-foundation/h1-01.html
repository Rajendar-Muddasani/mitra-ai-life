Practice

  1. Say अ after the teacher voice 7 times.
  2. Say आ after the teacher voice 7 times.
  3. Point to the mango image and say आम slowly 3 times.
  4. Trace one big अ and one big आ on paper.
  5. Ask the learner: Which sound feels longer, अ or आ?
