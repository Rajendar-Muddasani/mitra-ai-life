# Level 8: AI for Small Business

## Who this is for
Shop owners, home-based business owners, street vendors, small traders, kirana store owners, 
tiffin centres, tailoring shops — anyone running a small business in India.

## Lesson objective
Show a small business owner how to use AI to write better, market smarter, 
reply faster, and plan more confidently — without any tech background.

## Characters

### Ravi (returning from Level 1)
- Shopkeeper, Hyderabad
- Now uses AI for daily tasks (after L1)
- In L8 he takes the next step: growing his mobile accessories shop with AI

### Meena
- Runs a home tiffin service from Nalgonda
- 30 orders a day, no marketing, all word of mouth
- Wants more customers but doesn't know how to market

### Suresh
- Owns a small tailoring shop in Karimnagar
- Gets customer complaints via WhatsApp, struggles to reply politely
- Wants to write better and sound more professional

### Divya
- Young woman in Warangal, runs an online saree reselling business on Instagram
- Needs product descriptions, caption writing, and customer DM replies

## Story arc (5 acts)

### Act 1 — The Opportunity
Ravi's shop is doing okay. But the neighbouring big mobile store is stealing customers 
with slick marketing posters and quick replies. Ravi wonders: can AI help me compete?

### Act 2 — Write Like a Pro
Ravi uses AI to write a WhatsApp promotional message for Diwali offers.
Meena uses AI to write her tiffin service menu with prices and a tagline.
Lesson: AI writes professional text in seconds from simple instructions.

### Act 3 — Handle Customers Better
Suresh gets an angry WhatsApp message from a customer about a delayed order.
He types the complaint into AI and asks for a polite, professional reply.
Lesson: AI helps you reply calmly, professionally, in any language.

### Act 4 — Market Smarter
Divya needs Instagram captions for 5 saree photos. She describes each saree 
to AI and gets 5 ready-to-post captions with hashtags in 3 minutes.
Lesson: AI can do in minutes what took hours — product descriptions, captions, posts.

### Act 5 — Plan and Grow
Ravi asks AI to help him plan a 1-month WhatsApp marketing calendar for his shop.
AI gives him a weekly plan with message ideas, timing, and offer suggestions.
Lesson: AI is your free business advisor — not perfect, but practical.

## 5 skills taught

1. **Write promotional messages** — Diwali offers, new stock, sale announcements
2. **Reply to customer complaints** — politely, professionally, in Telugu or English
3. **Write product descriptions** — for WhatsApp catalogues, Instagram, local groups
4. **Create social media captions** — with hashtags, calls to action
5. **Plan a simple marketing calendar** — weekly WhatsApp plan, festival offers

## Practice challenges (quiz-style)
1. Ravi wants to announce a 20% discount on phone cases this Sunday. What prompt do you write?
   - a) "Write a WhatsApp message about my shop"
   - b) "Write a WhatsApp message for my mobile accessories shop announcing 20% off all phone cases this Sunday only. Keep it short and friendly."
   - c) "Tell me about discounts"
   - d) "Make a poster"
   → Answer: b

2. A customer messages Suresh: "My shirt is still not ready! You said 3 days, it's been 5!" 
   What is the best way to use AI here?
   - a) Ignore the message
   - b) Type back an angry reply
   - c) Paste the complaint into AI and ask for a polite, apologetic, professional reply
   - d) Close WhatsApp
   → Answer: c

3. Divya wants captions for a Pochampally silk saree. What detail helps AI write the best caption?
   - a) "Write a caption"
   - b) "Caption for saree"
   - c) "Write an Instagram caption for a Pochampally silk saree in orange and gold, for festive wear. Add 5 relevant hashtags."
   - d) "Make it viral"
   → Answer: c

4. Meena wants to grow her tiffin business. Which AI task gives the most direct benefit?
   - a) Ask AI about cooking recipes
   - b) Ask AI to write a WhatsApp message she can send to a local housing society group
   - c) Ask AI who the best tiffin providers in Nalgonda are
   - d) Ask AI to build her a website
   → Answer: b

5. Ravi asks AI for a marketing plan. AI gives suggestions. What should Ravi do next?
   - a) Follow it exactly without thinking
   - b) Share it publicly as a guarantee
   - c) Read it, adjust for his shop's reality, then try it step by step
   - d) Delete it — AI cannot help with real business
   → Answer: c
