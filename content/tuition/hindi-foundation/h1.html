Hindi First · H1 Sample Pack

Start From Zero, But Start With Confidence

This is the first real Hindi sample pack for the tuition track. It is designed for learners who feel weak, shy, or left behind. The teaching model is listen first, repeat slowly, connect each sound to a picture, and only then move to writing or testing.

How the media assets are planned

Image prompts

Every lesson has scene prompts for colorful teacher-safe visuals: large Devanagari letter, one anchor object, uncluttered background, child-friendly Indian context.

Video scenes

Every lesson includes scene-by-scene narration blocks: intro, repeat loops, anchor-image sections, tracing pause, and recap.

Quiz outlines

Every lesson includes the first five assessment questions so the learning flow can move from listening into recognition without pressure.

Parent note

This sample pack is intentionally slow. The goal is not speed. The goal is correct hearing, correct association, and reduced fear. If a child needs to repeat H1-02 or H1-03 many times, that is normal progress, not failure.
